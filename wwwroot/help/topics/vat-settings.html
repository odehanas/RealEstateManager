Input tax recovery

A business that lets both commercial property (VAT at 5%) and residential property (exempt) can reclaim VAT on its costs only in proportion to its taxable activity. Costs clearly belonging to one side are handled on the bill line — Taxable or Exempt. Shared overheads such as head-office costs are Residual, and for those a percentage is used during the year:

FieldMeaning
Provisional recovery rate (%)The share of residual input VAT reclaimed on each bill during the year. Normally last year's actual rate. 100 if all your lettings are commercial.

At year end the VAT Return works out the rate you actually earned and shows the correction (the wash-up).

Where VAT is decided
  • On rent — by the VAT category of the property or unit, copied to the contract and then onto each invoice. See Lease Contracts.
  • On costs — by whether the supplier is VAT registered, and by each bill line's attribution. See Vendor Bills.
