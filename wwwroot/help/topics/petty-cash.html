Petty cash is the small float kept on site to pay for minor items — cleaning supplies, a plumber's call-out, courier fees. Each float is its own asset account with its own running balance, so you always know how much cash should be in the box.

Setting up a float

  1. In System Setup ▸ Chart of Accounts, create an Asset account for the float — for example 11150 Petty Cash – Marina Tower. Then Edit it and set Petty Cash Float to Yes.
  2. Give it an Opening Balance if cash is already in the box on go-live, or fund it with a first replenishment.

Create one account per float if you keep cash at more than one site. Each appears in the float selector on this screen.

Recording an expense voucher

  1. Choose the float at the top of the screen, then select New Expense Voucher.
  2. Enter the Voucher Date, Paid To and a Description of what was bought.
  3. Choose the Expense Account the cost belongs in.
  4. Optionally choose the Property and Unit, so the cost appears against the right building in P&L by Property.
  5. Enter the Amount and any Reference Number (the receipt number), then save.

Replenishing the float

When the box runs low, top it back up from the bank with Replenish Float: enter the date, choose the Funding Account (normally your bank or main cash account), the amount and a reference.

Vouchers and replenishments post straight away

The float balance changes as soon as you save. If the posting is refused, the entry is kept as a draft and the message says why; post it from Operations ▸ Journal Inquiry once resolved - see Draft, Post & Reset to Draft.

A voucher is refused with "This voucher would take the float below zero" when the float does not hold enough cash. Replenish the float first, then enter the vouchers it pays for.

Counting the cash

The screen shows the float's opening balance, every voucher and replenishment in the date range, and the closing balance. Count the box and compare it with the closing balance. A shortfall means a voucher is missing or a slip was lost. Print gives a signed-off petty cash report.

Replenishing is not an expense

Moving cash from the bank into the box changes where the cash is, not how much the company has. The Statement of Cash Flows leaves these transfers out for that reason.

A voucher for an owner's property

For a cost of a managed property that the owner should bear, choose the property, tick Charge to the owner and choose the kind of cost. The voucher then charges the owner — plus 5% VAT when the company recharges, or as paid when it disburses (see VAT Settings) — instead of being our expense. See Costs passed on to owners.
