Payment approval makes sure no money leaves the company on one person's say. A payment is entered by one person and released by another whose authority covers the amount. The screens are under the Controls menu.

How a payment travels

  1. An accountant records a payment as usual — a vendor payment, a petty cash voucher, an owner settlement, an owner withdrawal or a development cost.
  2. If it is within their own limit (and the company allows that), it posts at once and is marked Released within own limit. Otherwise it is held: saved as a draft journal that has no effect on the accounts or the bank.
  3. It goes to the lowest approval level whose limit covers the amount. People on that level, or above it, see a red count beside Controls ▸ Payment Approvals and on Controls ▸ Business Performance.
  4. The approver opens it, checks the journal lines and selects Approve & release — the payment posts — or Turn down, with a comment saying why.
  5. The person who entered it is told the outcome. Every decision is kept with who made it and when.
Two rules that never bend

Nobody releases a payment they entered themselves above their own limit, and petty cash always needs a second person, however small the amount.

Payment Approvals: releasing

Controls ▸ Payment Approvals lists what is waiting: who entered it, the payment, the amount and the journal it will post. Show decided too adds the payments already released or turned down. Needs Release Payments or Owner Oversight.

Approval Limits & Delegation: setting it up (owners)

Controls ▸ Approval Limits & Delegation

  1. Payments need approval: switch the control on.
  2. The person entering a payment may release it within their own limit: leave it off and every payment needs a second person.
  3. Apply the limits to contracts too: the approver's level must then cover a contract's annual rent. Off, any contract approver may approve any contract (never their own).
  4. Levels: a new company starts with Accountant (1,000), Manager (5,000) and Owner (no limit). Change the limits or add a level; leave a limit blank for no limit. Select Save levels.
  5. People: put each user on a level, or No authority, and select Save assignments.

Payroll and salary advances post directly rather than waiting in the queue, so there the check is made on the spot: the person approving the payroll or recording the advance must hold a level that covers the amount.

Delegating while you are away

  1. Under Delegate authority while you are away, choose the person, an amount Up to (blank gives them your own limit), the From and Until dates and a reason.
  2. Select Delegate.

It ends by itself on the last day; End now ends it earlier. Delegations in force are shown on the Owner Monitor and the Authorization Matrix.

The Authorization Matrix

Controls ▸ Authorization Matrix is a printable statement of who may do what in the company: each role and what it allows, the approval levels with their limits and the people on them, delegations in force, how an approval travels, and every user with their role and level. Print it for your auditors or your policy file. Roles are changed under Users & Permissions.
