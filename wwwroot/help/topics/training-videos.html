Ten short videos show the main tasks of the system from start to finish. Each one is recorded in the practice company Al Waha Properties, so every name and amount in them is fictional. Watch them in order the first time: the lease in video 3 is for the tenant added in video 2, and so on.

Practise as you watch

Every new company gets its own practice company with a year of sample data - switch to it from the company list at the top. Anything you do there leaves your real records untouched.

Exercises waiting in the practice company

Besides a year of finished work, the practice company leaves some tasks for you to complete:

  • Approve a contract. A shop lease in Al Naeem Retail Annex waits under Master Data ▸ Approvals. Review it, then approve or return it.
  • Book a supplier's e-invoice. Two arrived under Operations ▸ Received E-Invoices. One supplier, Blue Wave Pest Control, is not set up yet - add it as a vendor first.
  • Correct a rejected e-invoice. One rent invoice came back rejected on Operations ▸ E-Invoice Monitor. Read the reason, then generate it again. Last month's are ready to send.
  • Post the drafts. A journal and a supplier bill are waiting in draft.
  • Set up depreciation for Marina Heights under Reporting ▸ Investment Property Note - the business centre shows how it is done.
  • Follow a unit sale. One shop has been sold and handed over; two flats in the Marjan Bay tower are sold off plan and still being paid for.
  • Read the Capital Assets Scheme record for Al Naeem Business Centre and its year 1 adjustment.
