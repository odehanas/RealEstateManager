Ten short videos show the main tasks of the system from start to finish. Each one is recorded in the practice company Al Waha Properties, so every name and amount in them is fictional. Watch them in order the first time: the lease in video 3 is for the tenant added in video 2, and so on.

Practise as you watch

Every new company gets its own practice company with a year of sample data - switch to it from the company list at the top. Anything you do there leaves your real records untouched.

1. Signing in and finding your way

Signing in, switching to the training company, the dashboard cards and the four menus.

2. Adding a property, a unit and a tenant

Adding a building, a flat in it and a tenant - the master data every lease builds on.

3. Creating a lease

Creating a lease, and how one contract creates all of its rent invoices.

4. Posting an invoice and receiving the rent

Posting the month's invoice, recording the tenant's payment and checking their statement.

5. Post-dated cheques

Recording post-dated cheques when they are handed over, and clearing them when the bank pays.

6. Entering a supplier bill and paying it

Entering a supplier's bill line by line with VAT, posting it and paying it.

7. Petty cash

Topping up the cash box from the bank and recording what is spent.

8. Month-end

Revenue recognition, depreciation, posting the month-end drafts and closing the period.

9. The reports

A tour of the Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, VAT Return and the tenant reports.

10. The tenant portal

Giving a tenant portal access, the tenant raising a request, and staff answering it.
