A security deposit is money you hold on the tenant's behalf and expect to return at the end of the lease. It is never income. The system records it as a liability from the moment it is received.

Recording a deposit

  1. Select New Security Deposit and choose the Lease.
  2. Enter the Reference Number, the Receipt Date and the Payment Method.
  3. Enter the Amount — normally the deposit agreed on the lease — and save.
  4. Post the draft journal from Operations ▸ Journal Inquiry.

Never treat a deposit as rent

Recording a deposit as a customer receipt would reduce the tenant's receivable and eventually show as revenue. Always use this screen. At the end of the lease, refund or forfeit the deposit with a manual journal that debits 21300.
