Signing in

  1. Open the application address in your browser. The Login page appears.
  2. Enter the Username and Password issued by your administrator.
  3. Select Login. You land on the Dashboard.

If you are a brand-new customer, use Create New Company on the login page. This registers a company, creates its first administrator, seeds the full chart of accounts and account mapping, creates the monthly accounting periods, and starts a 15-day trial.

Menus adapt to your permissions

You will only see the menus your account is entitled to. If a colleague can see a menu that you cannot, the difference is a permission, not a fault — see Companies, Users & Permissions.

Getting help on any screen

The Help button at the top right of every screen opens the instructions for the screen you are on, in a panel beside your work, so you can follow the steps without leaving the page. From the panel you can open the topic full-page, or go to the Help Center to search everything.

Switching company

If your user account has access to more than one company, a company selector appears at the top right. Choosing a different company reloads the current page in that company's data. Every list, report and balance in the system is filtered to the company you are currently in — you can never see another company's data by accident.

Changing your password

Select Password in the top-right corner, enter your current password, then the new password twice.

The Dashboard

Home shows the live operating picture: property and unit counts, occupancy rate, collections, outstanding receivables, liabilities and recent tenant activity. Use Print Dashboard for a clean printed snapshot.

The speedometer gauges

Four gauges show how the portfolio is doing against its targets. The needle points at today's figure; the dial is red, amber and green, with a tick where each colour starts, and the word under the figure says which zone it is in.

Each gauge links to the screen behind it. The same gauges appear where they help: occupancy, healthy tenancies and rent collected on each building in the Building View; collection ratio and average score on Tenant Health & Ranking; budget spent on a development project; and the share of the price paid on a unit sale.

Subscription status

If the company's subscription expires, a red banner appears and the Master Data, Accounts and Reports menus are withdrawn until an activation code is entered under System Setup ▸ Subscription. Read-only access to Setup remains so that the subscription can be renewed.
