The lease is the pivot of the whole system. A new contract is first saved as a draft, which can be corrected freely. The moment it takes effect - when it is activated, or approved if your company requires approval - it creates the entire schedule of rent invoices for its term and the month-by-month revenue-recognition plan, and its terms are locked.

Creating a lease

  1. Select New Lease.
  2. Choose the Unit and the Tenant.
  3. Enter the Start Date and End Date.
  4. Enter the Annual Rent for the full contract year.
  5. Choose the Billing Frequency (Months) — it must divide 12 exactly: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 12.
  6. Check the VAT treatment shown for the unit. Override it only with a reason (see below).
  7. Enter the Security Deposit Amount agreed in the contract and any Notes.
  8. Choose how to save:
    • Save and Activate - the contract takes effect at once (companies without approval).
    • Save and Submit for Approval - it goes to a second person (companies with approval).
    • Save as Draft - to finish it later. A draft has no effect on anything.

Approval in two levels

A company can require every new contract to be checked by a second person before it takes effect. Someone with the Approve Contracts permission switches this on or off in Master Data ▸ Approvals.

  1. The creator saves the contract and submits it for approval. Its unit is held: no other contract can be submitted for the same unit and dates.
  2. Approvers see it in Master Data ▸ Approvals, with a count in the menu and a note on the dashboard. Review shows the terms and every invoice it will create.
  3. Approve: the contract becomes Active, its invoices and revenue plan are created and the unit becomes Occupied. Return to creator (a reason is required): it goes back to Draft, with the reason shown on the Leases list, to be corrected and submitted again.
The four-eyes rule

Nobody can approve a contract they created or submitted, even with the Approve Contracts permission. Until it is decided, the person who submitted it can Withdraw it back to draft. Every submission, approval and return is kept, with who did it, when and why - see the history at the bottom of the contract.

The contract code

You do not type a contract number. The system issues one from the unit code, a tenancy number and the year, for example OWN-R-0001-U01-C001-2026:

  • The same tenant renewing the same unit keeps the tenancy number with the new year — …-C001-2027.
  • A new tenant on the unit receives the next tenancy number — …-C002-2027.

So a glance at the code tells you the building, the unit, which tenancy on that unit it is, and which year. See Master Data: Codes, Uniqueness & Who Can Edit.

VAT treatment

The VAT category comes from the unit, or from its property if the unit does not set one:

CategoryRateTypical use
Standard5%Commercial property — offices, shops, warehouses.
Exempt—Residential lettings after the first supply. No VAT is charged and VAT on related costs cannot be reclaimed.
Zero-rated0%The first supply of a new residential building. No VAT charged, but related input VAT can be reclaimed.

If a specific contract genuinely needs different treatment, choose a VAT category override and enter the reason. The reason is kept on the contract so the decision can be explained later.

What is generated when the contract takes effect

Invoice schedule. The annual rent is divided by the number of invoices per year (12 ÷ billing frequency). One draft invoice is created for each billing date from the start date until the end date. VAT is calculated per invoice from the contract's VAT category.

Revenue-recognition plan. One planned entry for the last day of every month in the term, each for one-twelfth of the annual rent. This spreads the rent evenly over time however it was billed or collected.

Unit status. The unit becomes Occupied and the lease Active.

Worked example

Annual rent 175,000, billed quarterly, one-year term starting 1 January:

  • 4 invoices of 43,750 each, dated 1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct.
  • 12 revenue-recognition entries of 14,583.33 each, at each month end.

After eight months, 116,666.64 has been recognised as revenue and 58,333.36 remains in Advances from Customers as deferred income.

An active contract's terms are locked

Its invoices were created from its rent, dates and billing frequency, so those can no longer be changed - only its notes. Check a contract while it is a draft. If commercial terms change later, end the lease and raise a new contract, then cancel any unposted invoices from the old one.
