Workflow step 1. A property is a building or a site. It is the top of the portfolio hierarchy and the anchor for every unit, lease, invoice and expense beneath it.

Creating a property

  1. Select Add Property.
  2. Choose the Ownership Type — this is the field that drives the accounting.
  3. Choose the Property Type: Residential, Commercial, Warehouse or Mixed Use. The screen previews the code the property will receive, such as OWN-R-0001.
  4. Enter the Name and Address. The name must be unique in the company.
  5. Choose the default VAT Category for its units — Standard for commercial, Exempt for residential.
  6. For a managed property, enter the Owner Name and the Commission Percentage you earn.
  7. Enter the Asset Value if you track the property's book value, then save.

Registration details

Below the main fields, the form has five sections for what the title deed, affection plan, permits and policies show. All are optional; fill in what you have.

SectionFields
LocationEmirate, area or community, plot number, land or municipality number, a location reference such as a Makani number, and GPS coordinates (the property page links them to a map).
Title Deed & LandTitle deed number and date, the authority that issued it, tenure (freehold, leasehold, musataha, usufruct, government grant), land use, plot area, your ownership share, and whether the title is mortgaged — with the lender and reference. The same deed number cannot be recorded twice in one emirate.
Building & CompletionBuilding permit number and date, completion certificate number and completion date, built-up area, floors, parking bays, developer, main contractor and consultant.
Utilities & InsuranceElectricity / water account, insurer, policy number and expiry date, and notes.
ValuationFair value from the latest independent valuation, its date and the valuer. Not booked — disclosed in the Investment Property Note.

Units have their own unit title deed number (for strata-titled units), parking bays and electricity / water account.

Why the completion date matters for VAT

The first sale or lease of a new residential unit within three years of completion is zero rated; later supplies are exempt. For an owned residential property the property page shows the last day of that window and how many days are left.

The property page and its documents

Select View in the list to open everything recorded about a property: the registration details, its units and its documents. To keep a scanned document with the property:

  1. Under Upload a Document, choose the type — title deed, affection / site plan, building permit, completion certificate, insurance policy, mortgage document, floor plan, lease registration and others.
  2. Enter its number, issue and expiry dates, and the unit if it belongs to one unit only.
  3. Choose the file — PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB — and select Upload.

Select the file name to open it. Documents that have expired are marked red, and those expiring within 60 days amber, with a warning at the top of the page; an expired insurance policy on the property is warned about the same way. Uploading and deleting needs Create & Edit Master Data; anyone who can view properties can open the documents.

Ownership type is decisive

Owned and Managed produce entirely different journals for the same invoice. Confirm the ownership type before you create any lease under the property — see Accounting Entries Reference.

Read only?

If you can see the list but not the Add and Edit buttons, your account has View Master Data only. Ask the master data owner to make the change.
