Account Mapping is the bridge between the operational side of the system and the ledger. When you post a customer receipt, the system needs to know which account is "cash" and which is "accounts receivable". Account Mapping answers exactly that question, once, for the whole company.

Defaults are applied automatically when a company is created, so the system works out of the box. You change a mapping only when your chart of accounts differs from the standard one — for example if you open a dedicated bank account and want receipts to land there instead of in 11100.

Changing a mapping

  1. Open System Setup ▸ Account Mapping.
  2. Choose the replacement account from the dropdown beside the mapping you want to change.
  3. Save.
Mapping changes are not retrospective

Changing a mapping affects future postings only. Journals already posted keep the account they were posted to. If you re-point "Default Cash" to a new bank account mid-year, the earlier balance stays in the old account and must be moved with a manual journal.

If a posting fails with a mapping error

A message such as "Account mapping for Default Cash is not configured" means the mapping is empty or points at an account that has been deleted or deactivated. Open Account Mapping, select a valid account, save, and retry the posting.
