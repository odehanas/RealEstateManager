The work queue for e-invoicing. Every posted invoice and credit note to a business or government tenant appears here until your provider has accepted it, ordered by its 14-day deadline: the file must reach your provider within 14 days of the supply.

The daily routine

  1. Post the month's invoices as usual in Customer Invoices. Those in scope arrive here as To generate.
  2. Select Generate all. Each file is built and checked. Anything that cannot be generated is listed with the reason — usually a missing TIN, licence or address on the tenant — and a link to fix it.
  3. Select Download ready files (ZIP) and upload them in your provider's portal.
  4. Back here, tick the documents you uploaded, enter the provider's batch reference if it gave one, and select Mark ticked as sent.
  5. When the provider reports back, record Accepted (with its reference) or Rejected (with its reason) under Sent — awaiting the provider's answer.

Sent means locked

Once a document is marked as sent it can no longer be reset to draft or changed. The law allows only one correction: a credit note. Mark documents as sent only after the upload has actually happened.

The warnings at the top

  • Past the 14-day deadline / Due within 3 days — send these first. Late issue carries a penalty of AED 100 per invoice, up to AED 5,000 a month.
  • Draft invoices with a passed supply date — the clock runs from the supply, not from posting, so post them promptly.
  • Setup is incomplete — open E-Invoicing Settings for the list.

Re-assess re-applies the scope rules to posted documents that have not been generated yet — use it after changing a tenant's customer type.

Each file is kept exactly as produced. XML downloads it again at any time, for your provider or an auditor; the e-invoice ID is also printed on the tax invoice.
