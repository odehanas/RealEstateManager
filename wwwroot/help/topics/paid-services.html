A paid service is work a tenant asks for and pays for. There are two kinds:

  • Special maintenance: work that is not the landlord's to do free of charge, such as an extra access card, a repair to the tenant's own appliance, or repainting to the tenant's taste.
  • Special request: a service the company may give to a subcontractor, such as deep cleaning, pest control or help with moving.

The company keeps a price list. The tenant orders from it, is sent a tax invoice, and, where the service says so, pays before the work is done. The job is then worked like any maintenance job, and the system shows what the service earned against what it cost.

Step 1: set up the price list

Open Operations ▸ Tenant Services ▸ Paid Services. For each service enter:

FieldWhat it is for
ServiceThe name the tenant sees and that prints on the invoice.
TypeSpecial maintenance or Special request.
Kind of workThe kind of maintenance work its jobs are sorted under, for example Cleaning.
What is includedA line the tenant reads when choosing.
Selling price (before VAT)What the tenant is charged. VAT is added where the company is registered. Enter 0 for a service priced on quotation: the price is then typed when the invoice is issued.
Expected costWhat the company expects to pay: the subcontractor's price, or materials and time. The list shows the expected margin.
Income accountThe account the price is credited to. Left empty, the company's Other Income account is used.
The tenant pays before the work startsWhen ticked, the job cannot be started or marked as done until the invoice is paid in full.
Done by a subcontractorA note for the office that an outside supplier does the work.
Offered to tenantsUntick to take a service off the list without deleting it.

Step 2: the order

  • The tenant, in the portal: the Services tab at the bottom of the portal. It lists the price list with each price including VAT; the tenant presses Order this service, chooses the property and a preferred date, and can add a note and photos. The tab shows only when the company has at least one service offered. The tenant's earlier orders are listed under the price list with whether each is paid.
  • Staff, for a tenant who called or wrote: Tenant Requests ▸ Log a Request for a Tenant ▸ kind of request Order a paid service.

Paid services are kept apart from ordinary requests in the portal. An ordinary maintenance request is free and is put on hold for a tenant with overdue rent or a returned cheque; a paid service is not held, because the tenant pays for it either way.

The order appears in Tenant Requests and in Maintenance Jobs with a Paid service box. It keeps the price it was made at: changing the price list later does not change it. An order changes nothing in the accounts.

Step 3: the invoice

Open the request. In the Paid service box check the price and press Issue invoice. The system raises a tax invoice to the tenant on their contract, numbered SRV-, and posts it:

AccountDebitCredit
Accounts Receivable (the tenant)Price + VAT
The service's income accountPrice
VAT PayableVAT

The invoice is in the company's own name and is the company's income whoever owns the building. It is never part of an owner's rent, settlement or profitability. The tenant sees the invoice and the amount due in the portal, and it is on their statement.

Step 4: the payment

Record the payment as for any invoice: Record the tenant's payment in the box opens Customer Receipts with the invoice chosen. Until the invoice is paid in full, a service that is paid before the work shows Work on hold, and Start work and Mark as done are refused with the reason.

Step 5: the work and its cost

Once paid, the maintenance team works the job as usual. Record what it cost on the job, under Materials:

  • Maintenance store or Bought for the job for materials, as on any job.
  • Subcontractor's charge for the supplier who did the work: the amount and the number of their bill.

The cost of a paid service is the company's, even in an owner's building, because the company earns the price. The subcontractor's bill itself is entered in Vendor Bills as usual; the line on the job is what links the cost to the service.

Orders and margin

Paid Services ▸ Orders and margin lists every order in a period with its invoice, what was paid, the cost recorded and the margin (price before VAT less cost), with totals by service.

Who can do what

The price list, the invoice and the orders report are open to users who manage tenant requests or the accounts, and to the company's administrator. The maintenance team sees the Paid service box on the job, with whether it is paid, but not the margin.
