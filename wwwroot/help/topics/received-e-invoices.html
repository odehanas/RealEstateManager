Your suppliers' e-invoices arrive through your provider as XML files. Upload them here and book each one as a vendor bill without retyping it: the supplier, their invoice number, dates and lines are filled in for you.

Receiving

  1. Download the files from your provider's portal.
  2. Open Operations ▸ Received E-Invoices, choose the files (several at once is fine) and select Upload.
  3. Each file is read and matched to a vendor by TRN, or by TIN for a supplier that is not VAT registered. The same document is never accepted twice.

If no vendor matches, the file is still kept and marked No vendor with this TRN. Ask whoever maintains master data to set the supplier up, with their TRN or TIN, then book it.

Booking as a bill

  1. Select Book as bill. The vendor bill form opens with the supplier's figures shown alongside.
  2. Choose the account for each line — the supplier cannot know how you classify their charge — and the property it relates to.
  3. Check that the totals agree with the supplier's, then save. The bill is a draft as usual; post it from Vendor Bills.

The received file is linked to the bill and kept as the evidence for your input tax claim.

Ignore sets aside a file you will not book — a duplicate sent by mistake, for example. It can be restored. Supplier credit notes are listed but not booked automatically: record them by reducing or reversing the bill they relate to.
