Workflow step 6. Rent invoices are created for you when the lease is saved. Your job on this screen is to post them when they fall due, print them for the tenant, and receive payment against them.

The invoice list

The list shows the invoice number, date, tenant, unit, subtotal, VAT, total, amount paid and status. Invoices generated from a lease begin in Draft: they exist as documents but have not touched the ledger.

Posting an invoice

  1. Locate the invoice in the list.
  2. Select Post. The journal is built and posted in one step, and the status becomes Posted.

The journal takes the invoice date, not the date you press Post. An invoice dated in a closed period cannot be posted.

Why invoicing does not create revenue

Billing and earning are different events. Issuing a rent invoice creates a receivable and an obligation to provide occupancy — not revenue. Revenue arrives month by month through Revenue Recognition, which is what keeps the Profit & Loss smooth even when a tenant is billed a year in advance.

Printing the invoice

The Tax Invoice button (or Invoice, when no VAT is charged) opens the printable document in a new tab.

DocumentWhen it is issued
Full tax invoiceVAT is charged and the tenant has a TRN on file. Shows both TRNs.
Simplified tax invoiceVAT is charged and the tenant has no TRN.
InvoiceNo VAT is charged — an exempt residential letting, for example.

Tax invoices are numbered in a single sequence for the whole company, as UAE law requires. The VAT treatment is fixed on the invoice when it is generated, so a later change to the property or unit never rewrites an invoice already issued. Your own TRN, legal name and address come from VAT Settings.

Receiving payment

Receive on an invoice row opens a customer receipt already pointing at that invoice — see Customer Receipts.
