Maintenance requests raised by tenants are worked as jobs by the maintenance team. Each job records who did it, when it was answered, started and finished, and the materials it used. The owner follows the whole operation on the Maintenance Dashboard.

Setting up the maintenance team

  1. Open System Setup ▸ Users & Permissions and select New User for the maintenance engineer or technician.
  2. Under Role, select Maintenance Engineer / Technician. This ticks only the permission Carry Out Maintenance Jobs.
  3. Grant access to the company and Save.

From then on, every new maintenance request from a tenant is assigned to a maintenance user automatically. With more than one, the job goes to the one with the fewest open jobs. If nobody has the permission yet, jobs wait unassigned and the Maintenance Jobs screen says so.

What the maintenance user sees

Operations ▸ Tenant Services ▸ My Maintenance Jobs

A maintenance user sees only the jobs assigned to them — not the accounts, not other requests, and not other technicians' jobs. The list opens on the Open jobs, worst first (Urgent, then High, then oldest); Done and All show the rest. The age turns red once a job is past its target time.

A notification for every new job

A technician who installs the app on their phone and turns on notifications (the card on My Maintenance Jobs) is told the moment a job is given to them, and when the tenant replies on it. Tapping the notification opens the job.

Working a job

  1. Open the job. The card shows what the tenant asked, the unit, the tenant's phone number, the preferred visit date and the timeline.
  2. Select Acknowledge — I have seen it. This is the first response the dashboard measures.
  3. Select Start work when you begin. (Starting also counts as the first response if you skipped acknowledging.)
  4. Record the materials used, as below, and add messages as you go. Notes are internal by default; untick the box for a message the tenant should read.
  5. Write What was done and select Mark as done. The tenant reads this text in the portal.

A supervisor — anyone with Handle Tenant Requests — sees every job, can Assign it to another technician, Reopen it, and Close the job once nothing more is expected. A technician cannot close a job.

Materials

Under Materials used, enter the item, quantity and unit cost, and where it came from:

Maintenance storeTaken from the items you keep for maintenance. The cost is posted at once out of account 11650 Maintenance Materials Stock.
Bought for the jobGive the bill or petty cash receipt number it was bought on. The item is recorded against the job; its cost is booked by that bill or voucher.

The maintenance store is a control account, not an inventory system

Buy stock for the store with a vendor bill (or petty cash) charged to account 11650. Items taken out for jobs reduce it. The balance is shown on the Maintenance Dashboard; a negative balance means items were issued before their purchase was booked.

The Maintenance Dashboard

Controls ▸ Maintenance Dashboard — for the owner (Owner Oversight), supervisors and report viewers.

Choose a From and To date (the last 90 days by default) and, if you like, one technician. It covers the jobs raised in the period and every job still open:

  • Jobs received, how many are done and declined.
  • Still open, how many are past their target and how many are unassigned.
  • Average first response — from the request to the engineer acknowledging it.
  • Average time to complete, and the share done within target.
  • Materials used and the maintenance store balance.
  • The open jobs, worst first, and breakdowns by technician, by property and by kind of job.
