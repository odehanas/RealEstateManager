Companies

System Setup ▸ Companies & Subscriptions — the platform owner only.

Each company is a completely separate set of books with its own chart of accounts, master data and ledger. Creating a company automatically seeds its chart of accounts, its account mapping and its monthly accounting periods.

FieldMeaning
NameAppears in the company selector and on reports.
Base CurrencyReporting currency, e.g. AED.
Fiscal Year Start Month1 for a January–December year. Drives accounting periods and the Balance Sheet's current-year profit calculation.

The same screen manages the company's subscription:

  • Generate Code issues an activation code for a number of days. It works once, for this company only. Send it to the customer; the history shows when it was used and by whom.
  • Cancel withdraws a code that has not been used yet - one sent by mistake or for the wrong period.
  • Suspend Company blocks the company's users at once, with a reason that is kept on record but not shown to the customer. Its unused codes are cancelled with it, so an earlier code cannot undo the suspension. Reactivate lifts it; so does a new code issued afterwards, once the customer has paid.

Users

System Setup ▸ Users & Permissions — system administrators, and each company's own administrators.

  1. Select New User.
  2. Enter the Username, Display Name and an initial Password.
  3. Grant access to one or more Companies. Tick Administrator of this company for someone who should manage that company's users.
  4. Choose a Role — Company Administrator, Owner, Manager (Approver), Data Entry, or Maintenance Engineer / Technician — to tick its permissions in one click, then adjust the Permissions if this person needs something different.
  5. Save and give the user their credentials. They should change the password at first login.

Who manages users

LevelWhat they can do
System AdministratorEvery company and every user. Set by the service provider.
Company AdministratorThe users of the companies they administer: add them, reset passwords, choose their permissions - including who approves contracts - and appoint other administrators of the same company. Whoever registers a company becomes its first administrator.
UserWhat their ticked permissions allow.
What a company administrator cannot touch

System administrators, and anyone who also works for a company they do not administer: changing that person's password would open the other company to them. Those users are managed by the service provider. An administrator also cannot deactivate themselves or remove their own administrator rights - another administrator of the company has to.

Keep Create & Edit Master Data to a few people

Master data is centralised so the same tenant or supplier is never set up twice. Give most users View Master Data, and Create & Edit only to the people who own the data. See Master Data rules.

Permissions are enforced at the server

Hiding a menu is not the control — the server independently refuses any request the user is not entitled to make. Typing a URL directly will not bypass a permission.
