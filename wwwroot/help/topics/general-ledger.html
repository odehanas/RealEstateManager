General Ledger

From any report figure to the ledger, and back

On the Trial Balance, Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet, every account code, name and amount (underlined with dots) opens the General Ledger of that account for the report's period. At the top left of the ledger, ← Back to … returns to the report you came from, and the buttons beside it open the Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss or General Journal for the same date.

Reporting ▸ General Ledger

The General Ledger shows the detailed movement of a single account between two dates. This is the report to use when you need activity for a period rather than a position at a date.

  1. Choose the Account.
  2. Enter the From Date and To Date.
  3. Select Refresh.

The report presents the opening balance at the from-date, then every journal line in the period with its date, entry number, description and debit or credit, with a running balance, and finally the closing balance and period totals.

Use this to explain any Trial Balance figure

If an account's Trial Balance figure looks wrong, run the General Ledger for that account across the full period. Every posting that produced the balance is listed, each traceable to its source document through Journal Inquiry.

Tenant Statement

Reporting ▸ Tenant Statement

A tenant-facing account showing everything charged to and received from one tenant.

  1. Choose the Tenant.
  2. Enter the date range.
  3. Select Refresh, then print or save to PDF for the tenant.

The statement shows the tenant's opening balance, each invoice as a charge and each receipt or cleared PDC as a credit in date order with a running balance, then total charges, total payments and the closing balance owed.

Vendor Statement

Reporting ▸ Vendor Statement

One supplier's account with you: what you owe them and how it arose. Use it to agree your balance with the supplier's own statement, or to answer "what have we paid them this year?".

  1. Choose the Vendor.
  2. Enter the date range.
  3. Select View Statement, then Print or save to PDF.

The statement shows the opening balance owed, then every posted bill (billed, a credit) and payment (paid, a debit) in date order with a running balance, then the closing balance you owe. Underneath, Open Bills lists the posted bills not yet fully paid at the end date, with the days overdue.

It is read from Accounts Payable itself, so draft bills and payments are not shown, and the closing balances of all vendors add up to Accounts Payable in the Trial Balance. Manual journals on Accounts Payable appear when the line was given the vendor.

Opening the original document

In the General Ledger, Journal Inquiry, the General Journal and the tenant and vendor statements, the entry number opens the journal entry with all its lines, and the source or reference opens the original document — the invoice, bill, receipt, cheque, payment, petty cash voucher, depreciation run and so on. From the journal entry page, Open original document does the same, and each account opens its ledger.

General Journal

Reporting ▸ General Journal

Every journal entry of a period with all its lines, its source and the document it came from. Filter by source (invoices, bills, payroll, manual journals…), status and a search text; a summary shows the count and total by source. Export CSV gives one row per line for a spreadsheet.
