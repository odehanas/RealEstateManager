General Ledger

Reporting ▸ General Ledger

The General Ledger shows the detailed movement of a single account between two dates. This is the report to use when you need activity for a period rather than a position at a date.

  1. Choose the Account.
  2. Enter the From Date and To Date.
  3. Select Refresh.

The report presents the opening balance at the from-date, then every journal line in the period with its date, entry number, description and debit or credit, with a running balance, and finally the closing balance and period totals.

Use this to explain any Trial Balance figure

If an account's Trial Balance figure looks wrong, run the General Ledger for that account across the full period. Every posting that produced the balance is listed, each traceable to its source document through Journal Inquiry.

Tenant Statement

Reporting ▸ Tenant Statement

A tenant-facing account showing everything charged to and received from one tenant.

  1. Choose the Tenant.
  2. Enter the date range.
  3. Select Refresh, then print or save to PDF for the tenant.

The statement shows the tenant's opening balance, each invoice as a charge and each receipt or cleared PDC as a credit in date order with a running balance, then total charges, total payments and the closing balance owed.
