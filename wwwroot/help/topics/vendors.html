Vendors are your suppliers and service providers — maintenance contractors, utilities, agencies. The vendor register is the payables sub-ledger and rolls up into 21100 Accounts Payable.

Creating a vendor

  1. Select Add Vendor. The vendor code, such as VEN-0001, is issued when you save.
  2. Enter the Name, Service Type, Contact Person, Phone and Email.
  3. Enter the Trade Licence Number.
  4. Enter the TRN, and tick VAT registered (active) only if their registration is currently live. For a supplier that is not VAT registered, enter its TIN instead, so its e-invoices can be matched to this record — see Received E-Invoices.
  5. Enter the Opening Balance only when migrating — positive means you owe the vendor. Save.

Why "VAT registered" matters

This tick box decides how every bill from this supplier is treated:

Vendor is…Their bills
VAT registered and has a TRNAre tax invoices. Taxable lines carry 5% VAT, which you can reclaim to the extent the cost relates to taxable supplies.
Not registeredAre commercial invoices. No VAT is calculated anywhere on the bill, and the whole amount is a direct cost.

A registered supplier must have a TRN on file — the system will not save the tick without one. Untick it when a supplier's registration is cancelled; bills already entered keep the treatment they were entered with.

No duplicates

A trade licence number or TRN can belong to only one vendor in the company. If you try to enter an existing supplier again, the save is refused and the existing vendor is named.

Accounting users can look vendors up to enter bills, but only users with Create & Edit Master Data can add or change one — see Master Data rules.
