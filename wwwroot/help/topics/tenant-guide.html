Welcome to your tenant portal. Here you can check your account at any time and send us requests, from your phone or computer.

Signing in

  1. Open the portal address you were sent.
  2. Enter the username and temporary password we gave you.
  3. The first time, you will be asked to choose your own password. Keep it private.

If you forget your password, or enter it wrongly too many times, contact us and we will send you a new one.

You can also sign in on the main Login page of the system with the same username and password.

Your account

TabWhat you will find
AccountHow much is due, anything overdue, the cheques we are holding, your deposit, and your tenancy details.
InvoicesAll your invoices and what is still outstanding on each. Overdue invoices are highlighted.
PaymentsPayments we have received, and the cheques you have given us with their status.
StatementYour full account for any dates you choose. You can print it.
ContractsYour tenancy contracts, rent and dates, and when each one expires.
RequestsThings you have asked us for, and our replies.

A payment can take a little time to appear while we record it. If something looks wrong, send us a request.

Making a request

  1. Open Requests and select New request.
  2. Choose what you need:
    • Maintenance / repair — something needs fixing. Choose the kind of problem and, if you like, a preferred visit date.
    • Request more time to pay — choose the invoice and the date you could pay by.
    • Contract renewal, Complaint or Other request.
  3. Give it a short title and describe it as fully as you can.
  4. If your account has an overdue amount or a returned cheque, maintenance requests are on hold: the page tells you so. Please contact our office to settle it, and you can then request maintenance again.
  5. Choose how urgent it is. Please use Urgent only for something unsafe or causing damage right now, such as a burst pipe or no electricity.
  6. Select Send request. You will receive a reference number such as REQ-2026-0001.

Following your request

Open the request at any time to see its status and our messages, and to reply. Its status moves from Submitted to Acknowledged when we have seen it, to In progress when work starts, and to Resolved with our response when it is done.

You can withdraw a request until we start work on it.

About requests for more time to pay

A request is not an agreement. The amount you owe does not change, and your current due date still applies unless we confirm otherwise. If we agree, you will see Agreed and applied on the request and the new due date on the invoice.

Get our replies on your phone

On the Account page, the App and notifications card lets you install the portal on your phone and turn on notifications. You then hear from us when we reply to a request or when its status changes, without signing in to check. On iPhone, first tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open the portal from there. You can turn notifications off again from Help.
