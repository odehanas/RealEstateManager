RealEstateManager installs from the browser, with no app store. Once installed it has its own icon on the home screen (or in the taskbar and Start menu on a computer), opens full screen without the browser bar, and can send notifications. It is always the current version: an update reaches every installed device the next time it opens.

Where to find it in the system

The App and notifications card offers whatever your device can do: install the app, turn notifications on or off. It appears on the dashboard (staff), the account page (tenants) and the home page (employees); Not now hides it there for a week. The same card is always at the bottom of the Password page (tenants: the Help page), and the phone button in the top bar goes straight to it.

iPhone and iPad

Apple does not let a website show an install button, so on an iPhone you install it through Safari's Share menu.

  1. Open Safari and go to the address above. (Chrome on an iPhone does not offer it on every iOS version; Safari always does.)
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (at the top on an iPad).
  3. Scroll the list down and tap Add to Home Screen. If it is not in the list, tap Edit Actions… at the bottom and add it.
  4. Tap Add. The RealEstateManager icon appears on the home screen.
  5. Close Safari and open the app from the new icon.
  6. Sign in again. The home-screen app keeps its own sign-in, apart from Safari's.
  7. Tap the phone button in the top bar, then Turn on notifications, and tap Allow.
On an iPhone, notifications need the home-screen app

They cannot be turned on in a Safari tab, and they need iOS 16.4 or later (Settings ▸ General ▸ About). In Safari, the card shows the steps above instead of the button.

Android

  1. Open the address in Chrome (Edge and Samsung Internet work too) and sign in.
  2. On the card, tap Install app and confirm. If the card is not shown, use the browser menu ⋮ ▸ Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open the app from its icon, tap Turn on notifications and tap Allow.

Windows or Mac computer

  1. Open the address in Chrome or Edge and sign in.
  2. Select Install app on the card, or the install icon at the end of the address bar.
  3. The app opens in its own window and is added to the Start menu (or Applications) and can be pinned to the taskbar or Dock. Turn on notifications from the card as on a phone.

What you are notified of

WhoNotifications
StaffA maintenance job given to you; a tenant's new request or reply; a contract waiting for your approval and its decision; a payment waiting for release and its outcome; a request to reopen a month; an employee's leave or document request — each to the people whose permission covers it.
TenantsA reply on their request, and each step it moves on: acknowledged, in progress, resolved, declined, closed.
EmployeesTheir leave approved, declined or cancelled; a requested document issued or declined.

Tapping a notification opens the page it is about. A notification shows a title and one line, so a locked phone never shows a page of figures. Signing out stops notifications on that device until you sign in and turn them on again.
