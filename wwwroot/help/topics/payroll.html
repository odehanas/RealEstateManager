The monthly payroll runs in three stages — prepare, approve, pay — and writes its own accounting entries. Preparing and paying needs Prepare & Pay Payroll; approving needs Approve Payroll.

Payroll Transactions: the month's additions and deductions

HR & Payroll ▸ Payroll Transactions

Anything beyond the fixed salary is a transaction: overtime, rest-day overtime, a bonus, a commission, another addition; unauthorised absence, late arrival, a penalty, a damage recovery, another deduction.

  1. Under New transaction choose the employee, the date and the Kind.
  2. Enter the Hours (or minutes or days) for kinds measured in time, or the Amount for kinds entered as money, and a description.
  3. Tick Approve now if you may approve it yourself, and select Save. Otherwise it waits; tick the waiting ones in the list and select Approve selected or Reject selected.
  • Generate from attendance creates the overtime, absence and lateness transactions from the month's attendance — see Attendance & Leave.
  • Overtime is priced at the overtime rate on the basic hourly wage (basic ÷ 30 ÷ daily hours); late minutes at that wage; unauthorised absence reduces the days paid, so it comes off the whole fixed salary.
  • The next payroll pays every approved transaction dated on or before its month end that no earlier payroll has paid. Draft payrolls update by themselves when transactions are approved, changed or removed.

Where payment approval is on, the person recording the advance must hold an approval level that covers its amount; otherwise it is refused and someone with that authority records it.

Step 1 — Prepare the payroll

HR & Payroll ▸ Payroll Runs

  1. Choose the Month and Year and select Prepare payroll. The run is numbered PR-year-month and calculated for everyone employed in the month.
  2. Check each line: days paid, basic, allowances, overtime, unpaid and absent days, additions, advance recovery, deductions and net.
  3. To change what one person repays this month, edit Advance on their line and select Save the advance recovery. To add overtime, a bonus or a deduction, use + / − Transaction on the line.
  4. If employee records changed since — a new joiner, a leaver, a salary change, newly approved unpaid leave — select Recalculate from employee records. Transactions already entered are kept.
  5. Print register prints the whole payroll for review. Delete draft removes a run prepared by mistake.

Fixed pay is pro rata to the days paid: someone who joined or left during the month, or took unpaid leave, is paid for their days only. Suspended employees are left out.

Step 2 — Approve

A user with Approve Payroll opens the run and selects Approve payroll. When the company has more than one payroll approver, the person who prepared the payroll cannot approve it. Where payment approval is on, the approver's level must cover the month's net pay. A payroll with a negative net salary is refused until the deductions are corrected.

The cost is posted on the day of approval when that falls inside the payroll's month, otherwise on the month end, so it always lands in its own month. Payslips become available — All payslips on the run, and each employee's own in their portal.

A mistake found after approval: Return to draft for a correction, provided nothing has been paid and the month is still open.

Step 3 — Pay salaries

Under Pay salaries the unpaid employees are grouped by how they are paid. Pay each group:

  1. Enter the Payment date (on or after the date the salary cost was posted) and the account it is Paid from. For cheques, enter the First cheque number; each employee gets the next number in the cheque book.
  2. Confirm the payment. It appears under Payments with its documents.
