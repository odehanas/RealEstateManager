The Profit & Loss reports performance over a period: revenue earned less expenses incurred between two dates.

Running the report

  1. Enter the As Of Date — the end of the period you want.
  2. Select Refresh. The report covers 1 January of that year to the as-of date.

What it contains

The report is laid out as an income statement. Each account sits on its income statement line, chosen on the account in the Chart of Accounts.

SectionContents
RevenueBy kind: rental income from owned properties, management commission from managed properties, property sales, other income.
Cost of revenueThe costs of earning that revenue: property operating costs, maintenance of owned properties, the cost of units sold.
Gross profitRevenue less cost of revenue.
Fixed costsThe same every month whatever the activity: salaries, office rent, depreciation, insurance.
Recurring costsRunning costs that move with the activity: utilities, office supplies, overtime, irrecoverable VAT.
Operating profitGross profit less fixed and recurring costs.
Finance costsInterest and bank charges.
Net profit / (loss)The result. This is the figure the Balance Sheet shows as Current Year Profit / Loss.

To move an account to another line — say, from recurring to fixed costs — open it in the chart of accounts and change its Income statement line. Totals do not change; only where the account is shown.

Managed properties

The rent of a managed property belongs to its owner and is not revenue here; only the commission is. Costs of managed properties are charged to their owners and do not appear here either — see Owner Reports.

Revenue appears only after recognition

Invoicing a tenant does not create revenue. If the Profit & Loss shows less rent than you expect, the usual reason is that revenue recognition has not been processed up to the report date. Run System Setup ▸ Revenue Recognition and refresh.

Non-calendar fiscal years

The report currently starts the period at 1 January of the as-of year. If your company's fiscal year starts in another month, the Profit & Loss period and the Balance Sheet's Current Year Profit / Loss will not agree. In that case, read the year-to-date result from the Balance Sheet, which follows the company's configured fiscal year start.

The Profit & Loss by Property report (Management Reports) does not have this limitation: it takes an explicit From and To date and defaults to the company's configured fiscal year. Use it when you need a period other than calendar year to date.

Analysing the result by property

This report presents the company as a whole. To see the same revenue and expenses broken down per property, use Reporting ▸ P&L by Property — see Management Reports.
