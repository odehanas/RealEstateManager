The Profit & Loss reports performance over a period: revenue earned less expenses incurred between two dates.

Running the report

  1. Enter the As Of Date — the end of the period you want.
  2. Select Refresh. The report covers 1 January of that year to the as-of date.

What it contains

SectionContents
RevenueEvery revenue account with a movement in the period, shown as credits less debits. Includes rental revenue released by revenue recognition, management fees and other income.
ExpensesEvery expense account with a movement, shown as debits less credits. Direct property costs, administrative costs, depreciation, finance costs.
Net Profit / LossTotal revenue less total expenses. This is the figure the Balance Sheet shows as Current Year Profit / Loss.
Revenue appears only after recognition

Invoicing a tenant does not create revenue. If the Profit & Loss shows less rent than you expect, the usual reason is that revenue recognition has not been processed up to the report date. Run System Setup ▸ Revenue Recognition and refresh.

Non-calendar fiscal years

The report currently starts the period at 1 January of the as-of year. If your company's fiscal year starts in another month, the Profit & Loss period and the Balance Sheet's Current Year Profit / Loss will not agree. In that case, read the year-to-date result from the Balance Sheet, which follows the company's configured fiscal year start.

The Profit & Loss by Property report (Management Reports) does not have this limitation: it takes an explicit From and To date and defaults to the company's configured fiscal year. Use it when you need a period other than calendar year to date.

Analysing the result by property

This report presents the company as a whole. To see the same revenue and expenses broken down per property, use Reporting ▸ P&L by Property — see Management Reports.
