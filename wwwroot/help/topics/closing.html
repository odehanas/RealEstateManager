Closing locks a period so that its reported figures cannot change afterwards. The screen shows the twelve monthly periods of the selected fiscal year with their status, and the history of fiscal years already closed.

The Closing Cockpit

System Setup ▸ Closing Cockpit shows the twelve periods of the fiscal year as tiles. Select a period to see its checklist. Each check compares what should be in the accounts for the month with what the general ledger actually holds, and shows a light:

LightMeaning
GreenDone, and the ledger agrees.
AmberA warning, such as the ledger differing from the schedule. You may close, but must tick that you have reviewed it; the warning is recorded with the close.
RedNot done. The period cannot be closed until it is resolved.

Closing a month, step by step

  1. Record the month's receipts, cheques, vendor bills and payments, payroll, and any accruals or adjustments as manual journals.
  2. Open System Setup ▸ Closing Cockpit and select the period.
  3. Work down the checklist. Use the button beside each red check, or follow its link to the screen where the work is done.
  4. Review the Trial Balance, Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet for the month.
  5. If amber warnings remain, tick I have reviewed the amber warning(s).
  6. Select Close. The tile shows a padlock.
Closing locks the period both ways

After a period is closed, nothing can be posted into it and nothing in it can be reset to draft, edited or deleted. Late documents go into the next open period — for vendor bills, use the Accounting Date. Corrections are made with a journal in an open period, or by asking for the period to be reopened.

Reopening a closed period

A closed period opens again only on a written request that an approver agrees to.

  1. In the Closing Cockpit, select the closed period and Ask for this period to be reopened. Say what is wrong and what will be posted to correct it, then Request reopening. You can withdraw the request while it waits.
  2. A user with the Approve Period Reopening permission opens System Setup ▸ Period Reopen Requests (the menu shows how many are waiting) and approves the request or turns it down, with a comment.
  3. Once approved, make the correction, then close the period again through the same checklist.

Every request, its reason, who decided it and when stay on the period's history. If the fiscal year had already been closed, reopening one of its periods undoes the year close: the closing entry is removed and the year must be closed again.

Closing the fiscal year

When all twelve periods are closed, the Year-end close panel at the foot of the cockpit shows the year's revenue, expenses and net result, and the balances that will be carried into the next year. Select Close fiscal year. The system posts one closing journal on the last day of the year that transfers the net result of every revenue and expense account into equity:

Close each year before reporting on the next

The Balance Sheet presents the current fiscal year's profit as a separate equity line and takes prior years from Retained Earnings. If a year is never closed, its profit stays in the revenue and expense accounts — which the Balance Sheet does not present — and the Balance Sheet will not balance once you cross into the following year.
