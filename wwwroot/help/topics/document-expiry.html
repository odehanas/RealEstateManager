One page that shows every document about to lapse, so nothing expires unnoticed.

Master Data ▸ Document Expiry (also under Controls)

What it lists

  • Tenants' Emirates IDs and passports that have expired or expire within the period you choose (30, 60, 90 or 180 days).
  • Other tenant and contract documents with an expiry date — visas, trade licences, registration certificates.
  • Property documents with an expiry date — insurance policies, permits.
  • Running contracts without an attested copy on file.
  • Tenants with a running contract and no Emirates ID expiry recorded.

Expired items show in red with how long ago they expired; items within 14 days show in amber. Each name opens the record, where the new document is uploaded.

Keeping it up to date

  1. When a tenant brings a renewed Emirates ID or passport, open the tenant and upload it under Tenant documents. The new expiry date is read and recorded — see Tenants.
  2. Upload each contract's attested copy under Contract documents — see Lease Contracts.
  3. Keep property documents on the property page — see Properties.
