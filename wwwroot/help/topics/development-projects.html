Operations ▸ Development Projects

A development project is a building under construction. Until it is complete, everything it costs — land, design, permits, contractors, supervision, borrowing costs — is collected in one asset account and stays on the balance sheet. Nothing is charged to profit and loss while the building is being built.

Setting up a project

  1. Record the plot as an Owned property in Master Data ▸ Properties, with its title deed and building permit.
  2. Select New Project. Enter the name, choose the property, the intended use (the account follows it), the main contractor and the dates.
  3. Enter the budget for each cost category and save. The project is numbered DEV-0001, DEV-0002 and so on.

Recording costs

Every cost line carries the project and a cost category. The project's cost to date is read from the posted ledger lines that carry it, so it always agrees with the balance sheet.

  • Supplier invoices (contractor certificates, consultants): enter a vendor bill and, on each line, choose the project and category in the box under Property. The line's account switches to the project's account by itself. Irrecoverable VAT, if any, becomes part of the project cost.
  • Everything else — land paid from the bank, a plot contributed by the shareholders, authority fees — use Record a Cost without a Supplier Bill on the project page. Choose the account it is paid from or moved from. It is posted straight away.
  • Adjustments: a manual journal line can carry a project too, but only on the project's own account.

What belongs in project cost - and what does not

Only costs directly needed to bring the building to completion: land and its transfer fees, design, permits, construction, infrastructure and utility connections, supervision, and borrowing costs on money borrowed to build it while it is being built (IAS 23). Marketing, sales commissions, show-flat running costs, general overheads and abnormal waste are expenses of the period, not project cost.

Following the project

The project page shows the budget, cost to date, remaining budget and percentage spent; budget against actual for each category; and the cost ledger — every posted cost with its date, source, supplier and category. Draft bills or journals tagged to the project are flagged until they are posted. On the project list, an amount posted to a development account without a project is shown as Not in any project so it can be corrected.

Completion

When the completion certificate is issued, enter its date and number under Completion. The date is copied to the property record, where it starts the VAT window below. Late invoices and retention releases can still be tagged to a completed project.

Transfer on completion

Once the project is complete, its cost leaves work in progress for where each unit now belongs. First add the building's units, with their areas, to the project's property (Master Data ▸ Units). Then, on the project page:

  1. Under Transfer on Completion, check the units, their areas and their share of the cost.
  2. Choose what each unit is for — for sale, for rental or own use. A mixed building simply has units of different kinds. Set every unit to changes them all at once.
  3. Enter the transfer date and, for rental and own-use units, the building's useful life in years.
  4. Select Transfer and Post. It is posted at once and cannot be reset to draft.

The cost is shared by area. The Land and acquisition costs category is kept apart from everything else (the building), so land is never depreciated:

The project page then shows the transfer, each unit's land, building and total cost, and links to the building's record in the fixed asset register. Depreciation starts in the month of the transfer. If a late invoice arrives after the transfer, a second transfer moves the remainder the same way.

VAT on a new residential building

The first sale or first lease of each new residential unit, made within three years of completion, is zero rated; later supplies of residential units are exempt. That is why input VAT on a residential project is normally entered as Taxable (recoverable) on the supplier bills. If the building is kept and rented out, the later exempt leases reduce that recovery over the following years under the Capital Assets Scheme — confirm the treatment of each project with your tax adviser.

Cancelling a project

A project can be cancelled only while nothing is recorded against it. An abandoned project's costs must first be written off with a journal.
