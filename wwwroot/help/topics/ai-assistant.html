The AI Assistant answers questions about your company's records and about how RealEstateManager works, by voice or by typing. It can also prepare entries for you — a receipt, a tenant, a journal — but it never saves anything itself: every change appears as a card that you confirm with a click.

Where it is

The round Assistant button at the bottom right of every screen opens the assistant panel. You only see it if your administrator has switched the assistant on and given you the Use the AI Assistant permission.

Asking questions

  1. Open the panel and type a question, then press Send. Or select the microphone and simply speak; the first time, the browser asks for permission to use the microphone.
  2. The assistant looks the answer up in your records and replies. When you talk, it answers aloud and the conversation is written in the panel as well.
  3. Ask follow-up questions in the same way. Mute pauses the microphone; End session or closing the panel finishes the conversation.

Examples: "Which tenants are overdue?", "How many units are vacant in Marina Heights?", "Show contract OWN-R-0001-U01-C001-2026", "What was the profit for September?", "Trial balance at 30 September", "Which cheques fall due this month?", "How do I close a month?". It understands English and Arabic.

Having it prepare an entry

Ask in plain words, for example "Record 5,000 received by bank transfer against invoice INV-…" or "Clear cheque 104207 today". The assistant runs the same checks as the screen — closed periods, balances, duplicates, approvals — and shows a Please confirm card describing exactly what will happen.

  • Confirm saves it through the same process as the screen and shows the reference, with a link to the record.
  • Cancel drops it. Nothing was saved.
  • A card left unconfirmed expires after ten minutes.
Saying "yes" is not enough

Only a click on Confirm saves anything. This is deliberate: a misheard word must never change your accounts.

It can prepare, each with its own confirm card:

AreaEntries
Master dataNew properties, units (many at once, codes generated), tenants and vendors
LeasingA draft contract, renewing a contract (a new draft from the day after it ends), sending a draft for approval or activating it
ReceivablesReceipts against invoices, receiving a set of post-dated cheques, clearing a cheque, posting a contract's draft rent invoices, a draft credit note
PayablesA draft vendor bill (with quantity and unit price), paying a vendor's open bills or an advance — payment approval limits still apply
Cash and accountingPetty cash vouchers, draft manual journals, posting a draft journal
Tenant requestsReplying to or updating a tenant service request

For each, it asks for anything missing, repeats the facts back, then shows the card. Ending a contract early is not in the system yet; the assistant says so.

Filling in the screen you are on

On any entry form — a new bill, contract, tenant, journal — say "fill this in" and give the details. The assistant reads the form, types the values (adding line rows if needed) and highlights them in yellow. It never saves: you check the fields and select the screen's own Save.

Opening a screen for you

Ask "Open a new unit for Marina Heights, 7th floor, 1,250 sq ft, rent 82,000". The assistant opens the screen from your menu with those fields filled in; a yellow banner says what was filled and anything it could not find. Opening a page ends the conversation, so the assistant asks its questions first. It only opens this system's own screens that your permissions allow.

Reading a scanned invoice

Select the paperclip and choose a supplier invoice (PDF or photo). The assistant reads it, repeats the supplier, number, date, lines and total with any warnings, and either fills the bill form you are on or prepares a draft bill for you to confirm.

Market rent benchmark

Ask "What is the market rent for a 2-bedroom apartment on Al Marjan Island?". The assistant searches the web and returns the typical rent, the range, the rent per sq ft, whether the figures are asking or contracted rents, and every source with its date — beside your own rents for the same kind of unit there and the gap in percent. The figures are indicative. A search is kept for a day.

What it will not do

  • Anything your own permissions do not allow — it tells you which permission or screen is involved.
  • Approve contracts or payments, delete posted records, reset posted entries, close periods, or manage users, permissions, companies or subscriptions.
  • See any company other than the one you are working in.

Privacy

Your questions, and the records the assistant reads to answer them, are sent to OpenAI to produce the reply. OpenAI does not use data sent through its business API to train its models. Passwords and sign-in details are never sent. The conversation and every confirmed action are kept in the system's audit records.

A scanned invoice, Emirates ID or passport you upload is sent to OpenAI to be read. A market rent search sends only the place and the kind of unit — never a tenant, contract or company figure.
