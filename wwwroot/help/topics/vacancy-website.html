The Vacancy Website is a public page of your company that shows the vacant units you choose to advertise. Anyone with the address can open it, on a phone or a computer, without signing in. It reads the same records you work with, so it is always up to date: a unit leaves the page by itself when it is let and comes back when it is vacant again.

Step 1: switch the website on

  1. Open Master Data ▸ Vacancy Website and press Settings.
  2. Tick Website switched on. Until then the address answers "not found".
  3. Check the address name: the last part of the link, for example /vacancies/al-waha. Choose it once; changing it later breaks links you have already shared.
  4. Enter the phone to call, and a WhatsApp number in international format (971501234567) to add a Chat on WhatsApp button to every unit.
  5. Optionally give the name and a short welcome text in English and Arabic, and press Save.

Use Copy to copy the address, and share it wherever you like: your WhatsApp status, social media, email signature, or a QR code on the building.

Step 2: prepare a unit's advertisement

The list shows every vacant unit. Press Edit advertisement on a unit and fill in:

  • Bedrooms and bathrooms (0 bedrooms means a studio), and whether it is furnished.
  • Asking rent per year. Leave it empty to show the unit's standard annual rent.
  • A description in English and, if you wish, in Arabic. A visitor reading in Arabic sees the Arabic text when there is one.
  • Features, one per line: Balcony, Sea view, Covered parking.
  • Photos: up to 12 for a unit. Choose several at once; large phone photos are made smaller before they are sent. The first photo is the cover shown on the unit's card; press Make cover on another photo to change it.

The area, floor, parking and location come from the unit and property records.

Step 3: put the unit on the website

Tick Advertise this unit on the website on the unit's advertisement, or press Put on website in the list. Take off removes it again.

On the websiteWhat it means
ShownThe public sees the unit now.
Ready - website is offThe unit is ready, but the website itself is switched off in Settings.
Hidden - not vacantThe unit is ticked but is occupied, reserved or under maintenance. It returns by itself when its status is Vacant again.
Not advertisedThe unit is vacant but you have not put it on the website.
What the public can and cannot see

Visitors see only units that are ticked, vacant and active, with the details on the advertisement. They never see tenants, contracts, owners, accounts or any other unit. With Show the rent on the website unticked, the rent is not printed and visitors are asked to enquire for the price.

Enquiries

Every unit has an enquiry form, and the front page has one for people who did not find what they need. What visitors send arrives under Enquiries, with a red count of the new ones.

  1. Open Enquiries. Each shows the name, phone, message and the unit asked about.
  2. Call the person, or press WhatsApp to open a chat with their number.
  3. Write an office note (what was agreed, when the viewing is) and set the status: New, Contacted or Closed. The name of who handled it and when is kept.
Who can do what

Users who manage leases or master data set up the website, prepare advertisements and answer enquiries.
