The Trial Balance lists every account with a balance as at a chosen date and proves that the ledger is internally consistent. It is the first report to run and the last one to check.

Running the report

  1. Enter the As Of Date.
  2. Select Refresh.

The report is cumulative: it includes every posted journal from the beginning of the ledger up to and including the as-of date, plus opening balances. That is the correct behaviour for a trial balance — it shows a position, not a movement. To see movement between two dates, use the General Ledger report (General Ledger & Tenant Statement), which shows opening balance, period debits, period credits and closing balance per account.

Posted journals only

Drafts are left out of this and every other report. If a figure is missing, look for a draft in Journal Inquiry.

Columns

ColumnMeaning
Code / AccountThe account, sorted by code.
TypeAsset, Liability, Equity, Revenue or Expense.
DebitShown when the account's net balance is on the debit side.
CreditShown when the account's net balance is on the credit side.

Each account appears once, with its net balance on one side only. Accounts with a nil balance are omitted.

Totals must be equal

Total Debit must equal Total Credit. Because the system refuses to post an unbalanced journal, any difference comes from opening balances, not from transactions. Go to System Setup ▸ Opening Balances and check the Net Imbalance card — the difference on the Trial Balance will match it exactly. Troubleshooting walks through the diagnosis.
