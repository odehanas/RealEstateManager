A credit note reduces or cancels a customer invoice: a rent discount, a lease ended early, tax charged in error. Under e-invoicing it is the only lawful way to correct an invoice once it has been reported — invoices can no longer be edited, deleted or issued with negative amounts.

Raising a credit note

  1. In Operations ▸ Customer Invoices, select Credit Note on the invoice.
  2. Enter the Credit note date. It decides which VAT return the reduction falls in.
  3. Choose the Reason from the statutory list, and write a one-line Explanation — it is printed on the document.
  4. Enter the Net amount to credit, or tick Credit everything still outstanding. VAT is reversed automatically at the invoice's own rate.
  5. Leave Reverse into account as it is to undo the invoice exactly, or choose a revenue account for a discount on rent already recognised.
  6. Select Save & Post, or Save as Draft to post later from Operations ▸ Credit Notes.

What a posted credit note changes

  • The invoice's balance falls by the credit. The invoice list shows it as credited under the amount paid, and a fully credited invoice shows the status Credited.
  • The VAT return for the credit note's period shows it as Less credit notes, reducing both the supplies and the output tax.
  • The tenant's statement, in the system and in the portal, lists it as a credit.
  • If the invoice was e-invoiced, the credit note is queued in the E-Invoice Monitor too. It can only be sent after the invoice it reduces.
Only what is still owed

A credit note cannot exceed the invoice's outstanding balance. Money the tenant has already paid is refunded, not credited. A posted credit note can be reset to draft until it has been sent to your provider; an invoice with a posted credit note cannot be reset until the credit note is.
