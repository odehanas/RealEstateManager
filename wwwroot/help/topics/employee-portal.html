Employees have their own small portal, made for a phone, at /me on your system's address. There they see their payslips, leave balance and salary advances, ask for leave and ask HR for certificates. Employees are not staff users: they have a separate login and cannot reach any company screen.

Giving an employee a login (HR)

  1. Open HR & Payroll ▸ Employees and select the employee.
  2. In the Employee portal panel, enter a Username — their mobile number or email — and select Give portal access.
  3. A temporary password is shown once. Pass it to the employee together with the portal address.
  • Reset password issues a new temporary password when one is forgotten.
  • Disable stops the login at once — for a leaver, for example; Enable restores it.

What the employee does

  1. Open the portal address and sign in with the username and temporary password.
  2. Choose a password of their own when asked: at least ten characters, with capital and small letters and a digit, and not the username.
PageWhat it shows
HomeAnnual leave left, last net salary, any advance still to repay, end of service to date, and the monthly package: basic, housing, transport, other and gross.
PayslipsOne payslip for every approved payroll, to view or print.
LeaveDays available, pending and taken; Request leave with the type, dates and a reason, then Send to HR; and the status of every request.
CertificatesAsk HR for a document — a salary certificate, a salary transfer letter or an employment certificate — saying who it is addressed to and why. Once HR issues it, the letter can be viewed and printed here.

Leave requests arrive under HR & Payroll ▸ Leave and document requests under HR & Payroll ▸ Employee Requests — see Attendance & Leave.

Three separate sign-ins

Staff sign in to the system itself; tenants to the tenant portal; employees to the employee portal. A login for one never opens another.
