The figures for your UAE VAT return for a tax period: VAT you charged tenants, VAT you can reclaim on costs, and the difference you owe. Use it to fill in the return on the FTA portal.

Running the report

  1. Enter the tax period From and To dates. The default is the calendar quarter you are in, the usual period for a business of this size.
  2. Refresh.

If no TRN is set, the report warns you first — see VAT Settings.

What it shows

SectionContents
Output Tax DueVAT charged on posted customer invoices in the period.
Input Tax RecoverableVAT you can reclaim on posted vendor bills in the period.
Output Tax — Supplies MadeInvoices grouped by VAT treatment (standard, exempt, zero-rated): number of invoices, net amount, VAT. Posted credit notes dated in the period appear under each treatment as Less credit notes and reduce both the supplies and the output tax. Our monthly commission tax invoices to landlords of managed properties appear as Standard rated - management commission to landlords (see Owner Settlements). Unit sales appear as … - sale of units, and Capital Assets Scheme adjustments under input tax (see Real Estate VAT).
Input Tax — PurchasesBill lines grouped by attribution (taxable, exempt, residual): number of bills, net amount, VAT charged, recoverable and irrecoverable.
Recovery RateThe actual recovery rate for the period — taxable supplies as a share of all supplies made — with the provisional rate from VAT Settings shown underneath for comparison.

Net VAT payable is Output Tax Due less Input Tax Recoverable. A negative figure is a refund due to you.

Only posted documents count

Draft invoices and draft bills are left out, because they are not in the accounts. Post everything for the period before you run the return. Bills from suppliers who are not VAT registered carry no VAT and do not appear in the input tax figures at all.

Owners' rent and our recharges

Rent invoiced in an owner's name is the owner's supply and is left out of this return. Costs we recharge to owners are our own standard-rated supplies and appear as Standard rated – costs recharged to owners. See Costs passed on to owners.

The annual apportionment wash-up

Residual input VAT is reclaimed during the year at the provisional recovery rate. At the end of the fiscal year the report compares that with the actual recovery rate — your taxable supplies as a share of all supplies for the year — and shows the difference:

  • A positive figure is additional VAT you may reclaim.
  • A negative figure is VAT reclaimed too generously that must be repaid.

This figure is not posted automatically. Enter it on the return for the first period after year end, and record it with a manual journal between 11300 VAT Recoverable and the expense account the residual costs were charged to. Then update the provisional rate in VAT Settings for the new year.

Settling with the FTA

When you pay the VAT due, post a manual journal: Dr 21400 VAT Payable for the output tax, Cr 11300 VAT Recoverable for the input tax, Cr 11100 Cash for the net amount paid.

Owner VAT Return

Reporting ▸ Owner VAT Return

The VAT position of a VAT-registered owner of managed properties, for them to file under their own TRN. Choose the owner (only owners with a TRN are listed) and the tax period — the calendar quarter by default.

PartWhat it contains
Output taxRent invoiced in the owner's name (see invoicing in the owner's name), by VAT category, less credit notes against it.
Input taxThe company's tax invoices to the owner — management commission and recharged costs — and the supplier's VAT on costs the company paid on the owner's behalf (disbursements).
Net VATPayable when output exceeds input, refundable otherwise.

Every document is listed and opens. If some of the owner's properties are still invoiced by the company, a note says so: that rent is on the company's return, not the owner's. The owner adds any other income or costs of theirs that are outside this system.
