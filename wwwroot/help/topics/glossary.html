TermMeaning
Accrual basisRecording income when it is earned and expenses when they are incurred, regardless of when cash moves. The basis this system uses.
Advances from CustomersAccount 21200. Rent billed but not yet earned. A liability, released to revenue month by month.
Control accountA GL account whose balance is the total of a sub-ledger — Accounts Receivable over tenants, Accounts Payable over vendors.
Double entryEvery transaction affects at least two accounts with equal debits and credits, so the ledger is always self-proving.
Due to LandlordsAccount 21500. Rent collected on a managed property that belongs to its owner.
Fiscal yearThe company's twelve-month reporting year, starting in the configured fiscal year start month.
Managed propertyA property you administer for a third-party owner. Only your commission is revenue.
Natural balanceThe side an account normally sits on: debit for assets and expenses, credit for liabilities, equity and revenue.
Opening balanceA balance brought forward from a previous system at go-live.
Owned propertyA property you own. The rent is your revenue.
PDCPost-dated cheque — a cheque held today, payable on a future date.
PostingWriting a document's journal into the general ledger. Before posting, a document has no accounting effect.
Revenue recognitionReleasing billed rent into revenue as the occupancy is provided, one month at a time.
Sub-ledgerA detailed register behind a control account — the tenant register, the vendor register, the fixed asset register.
Trial balanceA listing of every account balance at a date, proving total debits equal total credits.
Unearned revenueSee Advances from Customers.
Accounting dateOn a vendor bill, the date its journal is booked to, which decides the period. Usually the bill date; later when the bill arrives after its month has closed.
Commercial invoiceA supplier invoice that carries no VAT, because the supplier is not VAT registered. The whole amount is a cost.
DraftA journal that has been saved but not posted. It has no effect on any report until posted.
Input tax attributionHow a cost relates to your supplies — Taxable, Exempt or Residual — which decides how much of its VAT you can reclaim.
Internal noteA message on a tenant request that only staff can see.
Petty cash floatA small sum of cash kept on site, held in its own asset account.
PostPut a draft journal into the accounts, so the reports include it.
Reset to DraftTake a posted journal back out of the accounts so its document can be corrected.
Tax invoiceAn invoice that charges VAT and meets UAE requirements. Full when the customer has a TRN, simplified when not.
Tenant portalThe separate site where tenants see their own account and send requests.
TRNTax Registration Number, issued by the Federal Tax Authority to a VAT-registered business.
Wash-upThe year-end correction of input VAT reclaimed on shared costs, from the provisional rate to the rate actually earned.
