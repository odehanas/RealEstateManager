The Balance Sheet reports financial position at a single date: what the company owns, what it owes, and the residual belonging to the owners.

Running the report

  1. Enter the As Of Date.
  2. Select Refresh.

Structure

SectionContents
AssetsCash, receivables, VAT recoverable, PDCs on hand, prepaid expenses, investment property, fixed assets net of accumulated depreciation.
LiabilitiesPayables, advances from customers (deferred rent), security deposits held, VAT payable, amounts due to landlords, PDC contra.
EquityShare Capital, Retained Earnings – Prior Years, any other equity accounts, and Current Year Profit / Loss computed from the fiscal-year-to-date Profit & Loss.

The balancing check

Total Assets must equal Total Liabilities and Equity. If they differ, the cause is almost always one of:

  • Unbalanced opening balances. Check System Setup ▸ Opening Balances; the difference will match the Net Imbalance exactly.
  • An unclosed prior fiscal year. Prior-year profit remains in revenue and expense accounts, which the Balance Sheet does not present. Close the year (Period & Year Closing).
Cross-check against the Trial Balance

Trial Balance total debit minus total credit equals Total Assets minus Total Liabilities and Equity. The two reports fail together and are fixed together — you never have to diagnose them separately.
