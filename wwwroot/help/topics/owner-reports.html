Two reports written for property owners: what is let to whom in their buildings, and what they have personally drawn from the business.

Owner Tenancy Schedule

Reporting ▸ Owner Tenancy Schedule

Who occupies each unit, on what terms and what they owe, grouped by property owner. It is the report to send an owner of a managed building, or to review your own portfolio unit by unit.

  1. Enter the As Of Date.
  2. Optionally narrow it to Owned or Managed property, or to one Owner.
  3. Tick Show vacant units to list empty units too — useful for seeing what is available to let.
  4. Refresh, then print for the owner.

Each row shows the property and unit, the tenant and their contact details, the contract and its term, the rent and VAT treatment, what is outstanding, and the contract status. The owner name comes from the property record.

Owner Withdrawals

Reporting ▸ Owner Withdrawals

Enter a From and To date — the default is the company's fiscal year to date. The report has three parts:

  • Owner Account Movement — per owner account: opening balance, withdrawals and contributions in the period, and closing balance.
  • Breakdown by Category — categories down the rows, owner accounts across the columns, with each category's share of total spend.
  • Detail — every line posted to an owner account in the period, with its date, entry number, description and category.
ReadingMeaning
Credit closing balanceThe business still owes the owner this amount.
Debit closing balanceThe owner has drawn more than they put in and now owes the business.
Negative category amountA refund of personal spend back into the business, posted against that category.
Uncategorised rowMovement with no category. Expected for capital introduced or a transfer of the owner's balance; investigate if it represents spending.
One posting, two truthful reports

The owner gets a full personal expenditure statement, and the company's Profit & Loss stays a true record of trading. Both come from the same posted journal, so there is no separate spreadsheet to reconcile. See Owner Withdrawals Explained.

Owner Property Profitability

Reporting ▸ Owner Property Profitability

For each managed property, what it earned its owner in the period and what it earned the company. Choose the dates (the fiscal year to date by default) and, if you like, one owner.

ColumnMeaning
LetUnits let out of units in the property.

VAT on commissionCharged to the owner on our commission tax invoice.

Owner's net incomeRent billed less commission, its VAT and costs — and the owner's margin on the rent.
