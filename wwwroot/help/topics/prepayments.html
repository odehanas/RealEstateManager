Use a prepayment schedule when you pay once for something consumed over many months — annual insurance, a trade licence, a maintenance contract. The cost is parked on the balance sheet and released to the Profit & Loss evenly.

Creating a schedule

  1. Select New Prepayment.
  2. Enter a Description and choose the Prepayment Type.
  3. Enter the Payment Date and the Total Amount.
  4. Enter the First Recognition Date (normalised to a month end) and the Recognition Months.
  5. Choose the Prepaid Account (default 11500) and the Expense Account the cost belongs in.
  6. Choose the Offset Account — cash if you paid directly, or Accounts Payable with a Vendor if it is on credit.
  7. Save.

Saving creates the setup journal as a draft; post it from Journal Inquiry:

AccountDebitCredit
11500 Prepaid ExpensesTotal amount—
Offset — 11100 Cash or 21100 Accounts Payable—Total amount

Monthly amortisation

Each month end, select Post All Due Up To Selected Date to release the instalments due, then post the resulting drafts from Journal Inquiry:

AccountDebitCredit
Expense account chosen on the scheduleMonthly amount—
11500 Prepaid Expenses—Monthly amount

When the final instalment posts, the schedule is marked Completed and the prepaid balance for it is nil.

Prepayment postings cannot be reset to draft

Once posted, set-up and amortisation entries create and consume a schedule, so they cannot simply be reset. To correct one, post a reversing manual journal.
