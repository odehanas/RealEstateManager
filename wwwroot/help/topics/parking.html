Car Parking keeps each property's numbered parking bays, who has each one, and which are free. A bay is given to a contract, and so to that contract's unit and tenant, for a charge billed on the payment terms you choose.

Step 1: add the bays of a property

Open Master Data ▸ Car Parking. Under Add parking bays choose the property, then either:

  • One bay: type its number (for example P-7) and leave From and To empty.
  • A run of bays: type a prefix and the first and last number. B1- from 1 to 40 makes B1-01 to B1-40. Numbers the property already has are skipped.

The kind of bay (Covered, Uncovered, Basement, Visitor) is optional.

Step 2: give a bay to a contract

  1. On a vacant bay press Give to a contract.
  2. Choose the contract. Only contracts in force on that property are offered.
  3. Set the date it starts from. The bay runs until the contract ends.
  4. Enter the charge a year before VAT, or 0 when the bay comes with the unit free of charge.
  5. Choose the payment terms: One payment, or Monthly, Every 2 Months, Quarterly, Every 4 Months, Semi-Annual or Annual, the same choices as a contract's rent.
  6. Check the VAT. It starts as the contract's own treatment and can be changed.
  7. Add the vehicle plate if you record it, and press Give the bay.

How the charge is billed

Payment termsWhat is created
One paymentOne invoice on the start date for the whole period: the yearly charge for the months it covers.
Monthly, Quarterly and the othersAn invoice on the start date and then every so many months until the contract ends, each for the yearly charge divided by the number of payments in a year.
Charge of 0No invoice. The bay shows as occupied and free.

The invoices are numbered PRK-, read Car parking, bay …, and are created as drafts, like a contract's rent invoices: post each from Customer Invoices when it falls due. The tenant sees them in the portal and on their statement.

Parking is rent of the property

A parking charge is accounted for exactly as rent is. For a property the company owns it is the company's rental income, earned month by month. For a property managed for an owner it is the owner's money: it enters the owner's settlement and profitability, and the company earns its commission on it.

The board

Each property shows its bays as tiles: green for vacant, blue for occupied (with the tenant, unit, vehicle plate, end date and yearly charge) and grey for closed. The figures at the top count the bays, how many are occupied and vacant, and the parking charges a year for the bays now taken. Use the property filter to see one building, and Print for a paper copy.

Taking a bay back

  • Release on an occupied bay frees it from the date you choose. Draft parking invoices dated after that day are removed. Invoices already posted stay; issue a credit note if the tenant is owed a refund.
  • When a contract is closed through a Lease Handover, its bays are released automatically from the handover date.
  • Close takes a vacant bay out of use without deleting it; Open again brings it back.
Who can do what

Users who manage leases or master data add bays and give or release them. Users who can view master data, and the owner, can open the board.
