HR & Payroll ▸ Employees

The staff register: who works for the company, their contract and UAE documents, what they are paid and how. Everything the payroll, leave and attendance screens do starts from these records. The HR & Payroll menu is shown to users with Manage Employees, Prepare & Pay Payroll or Approve Payroll.

Before the first employee

  1. HR & Payroll ▸ Departments: type each department's name (for example Building Maintenance) and select Add. A department no longer used is deactivated, not deleted.
  2. HR & Payroll ▸ Payroll Settings: the WPS details, the accounts and the calculation rules — see Payroll.

Adding an employee

  1. Open HR & Payroll ▸ Employees and select Add Employee. The employee code (EMP-0001, EMP-0002 …) is generated on save.
  2. Personal: name in English and Arabic, gender, date of birth, nationality, mobile, email and an emergency contact.
  3. Job and contract: job title, department, joining date, end of probation, the contract type (Unlimited, Limited, Part-time or Temporary) and, for a limited contract, its end date.
  4. Works for building: choose a property when the person works for one building — a watchman or a cleaner, for example. Their salary is then charged to that building and appears in P&L by Property. Leave it at Head office / none otherwise.
  5. UAE documents: passport, Emirates ID, residence visa, labour card and health insurance, each with its expiry date, and the 14-digit MOHRE person code from the labour card, needed for the WPS salary file.
  6. Salary and payment: basic salary, housing, transport and other allowances; and Paid by — WPS, bank transfer or cheque — with the bank, IBAN and the bank's 9-digit routing code for WPS.
  7. Leave and end of service: annual leave days a year (30 under UAE law after the first year).
  8. Select Save.
Staff moved from a previous system

Enter Balances as at, the Opening leave balance in days and the Opening gratuity provision already in your books at that date. Leave then accrues, and the provision is topped up, from that date onwards. For new joiners leave these blank. Many employees can be loaded at once from Excel — see Importing from a Previous System.

The employee page

Select an employee in the list to see everything about them in one place:

  • Monthly gross, split into basic and allowances.
  • Annual leave available, and any days waiting for approval.
  • End-of-service gratuity today, how it is worked out, and how much of it is already provided in the accounts.
  • Their documents with expiry dates, their leave, payslips and salary advances.
  • The Employee portal panel, where you give them a login — see Employee Self-Service.

End-of-service gratuity

Worked out as UAE law gives it, on the last basic salary: 21 days' basic pay for each of the first five years of service and 30 days for each year after, part years pro rata, never more than two years' pay. Nothing is payable for less than one year's service, but the full amount is provided for in the accounts from the first day, month by month, by the payroll.

Expiring documents

HR & Payroll ▸ Expiring Documents lists passports, Emirates IDs, visas, labour cards, insurance and limited contracts of current staff that have expired or expire within the chosen number of days, soonest first, ready to print.
