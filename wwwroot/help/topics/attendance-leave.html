Attendance and leave are what make one month's pay differ from the next: overtime adds to it; unpaid leave, absence and lateness reduce it. Record them here and the payroll picks them up by itself. Both screens need Manage Employees.

Leave

HR & Payroll ▸ Leave

  1. Under Book leave, choose the employee, the type and the From and To dates, add a reason and select Book. Days are counted as calendar days, both dates included. Employees can also ask for leave themselves from the employee portal; their request arrives here as Pending.
  2. On a pending request select Approve or Reject, with a comment if you wish.
  3. Approved leave that will not be taken after all: Cancel leave.
TypeEffect when approved
AnnualComes off the employee's annual leave balance. Paid as usual.
UnpaidThe days are deducted by the payroll of the month they fall in. Leave that spans two months is split between them.
Sick, Maternity, Parental, Compassionate, Hajj, OtherRecorded and shown on the attendance sheet as On leave. No effect on the annual balance or the pay.

The annual leave balance is the opening balance, plus leave earned month by month at the employee's yearly entitlement, less approved annual leave taken. It is shown on the employee's page and in their portal.

Attendance: the day sheet

HR & Payroll ▸ Attendance

  1. Choose the Date. Everyone employed that day is listed. People not yet recorded start as On leave (when approved leave covers the day), Weekly off (on the company's rest day) or Present.
  2. Set each person's Status and, if you record times, In and Out. Hours worked, overtime and minutes late are worked out from the times.
  3. Select Save the day.
  • Absent means absent without leave. It becomes an unauthorised-absence deduction.
  • Times entered on a weekly off or a holiday are paid as rest-day overtime.
  • On a working day, overtime is the time beyond the normal working day set in Payroll Settings.

Importing from a time-attendance device

Under Import device file (CSV), choose the file exported from the device and select Import. The file has one line per employee per day:

ColumnFormat
EmployeeCodeThe code from the employee register, e.g. EMP-0007
Dateyyyy-MM-dd or dd/MM/yyyy
CheckIn, CheckOutHH:mm

A header line is skipped. Someone clocked in is present; someone clocked in on a rest day is recorded as working on their weekly off.

From attendance to pay

  1. Select Month summary & payroll. Each employee's month is summarised: days present, absent, on leave, rest days, days not recorded, late days and minutes, overtime hours and rest-day overtime hours.
  2. Check it. Working days with nothing recorded are treated as worked; complete the day sheets first if that is not right.
  3. Select Generate payroll transactions. One approved transaction is made per employee and kind: overtime, rest-day overtime, unauthorised absence and — if Deduct late time from pay is on in Payroll Settings — late arrival.
  4. Select See the transactions to review them under Payroll Transactions.

Generating again replaces what was generated before, so attendance can be corrected and re-run until the month's payroll is approved.

Certificates and letters employees ask for

HR & Payroll ▸ Employee Requests lists the salary certificates, salary transfer letters and employment certificates employees asked for in their portal, with who the letter is addressed to and why.

  • Issue gives the letter a reference number. You can Print it here, and the employee can open and print it from their phone.
  • Decline, with a reason.
