Operations ▸ Unit Sales

Selling units — completed or off plan — with a sale and purchase agreement, a payment plan, the buyer's payments and the handover. Revenue is recognised at handover: until then, everything the buyer pays is held as an advance.

Signing a contract

  1. Add the buyer to the customer register first (Master Data ▸ Tenants), with their Emirates ID or passport and, for a company, its TRN.
  2. Select New Sale Contract. Choose the unit, the buyer, the contract date and the sale price before VAT. The VAT treatment appears at once, with the reason.
  3. Enter the payment plan — up to six instalments with due dates, amounts including VAT — or leave it empty for one payment of the full price. Split 20 / 30 / 30 / 20 fills a typical off-plan plan. The plan must add up to the price including VAT.
  4. Select Sign Contract. It is numbered SPA-year-0001; the unit becomes Reserved.

The VAT treatment

UnitTreatment
Commercial (standard rated unit or property)Standard rated, 5%
Residential, the unit's first supply, within three years of completion — or before completion (off plan)Zero rated
Residential, any later supply (resale, or sold after it was first let)Exempt

A building is treated as new when its property has a completion date or a development project. The decision and its reason are kept on the contract, and the sale is recorded as the unit's first supply. See Real Estate VAT.

Recording the buyer's payments

On the contract page, under Record a Payment, enter the date, amount (including VAT), method and the bank account. It is posted at once. The payment plan shows each instalment as paid, part paid, due or overdue.

Handover

The unit must have its cost in 13200 Completed Properties Held for Sale — that is, its development project is complete and the unit was transferred for sale. Enter the handover date and, if known, the buyer's new title deed number, and select Hand over:

The unit becomes Sold; the contract page shows revenue, cost of sales and gross profit. Payments and the handover cannot be reset to draft — a correction is a journal.

Cancelling

A contract can be cancelled only while nothing has been paid; the unit becomes available again. If the buyer has paid, agree the refund or forfeiture and record it with a journal first.
