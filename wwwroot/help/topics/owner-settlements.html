Operations ▸ Owner Settlements

For a managed property we collect the rent on the landlord's behalf. The landlord is owed that rent less our commission and the VAT on it. An owner settlement is the payment of that money to the landlord, in full or in part, with a settlement advice to hand over.

How the amount owed is worked out

Rent billed to the tenants up to the statement date (before VAT, after credit notes)A
Less: our commission at the property's Commission %(B)
Less: 5% VAT on that commission (when your company is VAT registered)(V)
Owner's shareA − B − V
Less: settlements already paid to the owner(C)
Balance payable to the ownerA − B − V − C

Only posted invoices, credit notes, receipts and cleared cheques count. VAT charged to the tenants is never part of the owner's money — it is paid to the FTA. The statement also shows the total rent of each agreement for its full term, for reference.

Next to the balance you see Available from collections: the owner's share of rent the tenants have actually paid, less what was already settled. That is the cash you hold for the owner. You may pay more, up to the full balance, but the difference is an advance of rent the tenant still owes you.

VAT on our commission: the monthly commission tax invoice

Our commission is a service we supply to the landlord — property management. It is standard rated at 5% even when the rent itself is exempt residential rent: the exemption covers the lease between landlord and tenant, not our fee. So every month each landlord receives a tax invoice from us for the commission plus VAT, and that amount is taken from the rent we collected for them.

  1. Post the month's revenue recognition first (System Setup ▸ Revenue Recognition). That is what moves the month's commission into Management Fee Revenue.
  2. Open Operations ▸ Owner Settlements. Under Commission Tax Invoices the month just ended is offered, with the commission waiting to be invoiced per owner. Choose another Month ending and select Check if needed.
  3. Select Issue Commission Invoices. One invoice per landlord is created, dated the month end, with a number from your tax invoice series (TX-year-…, the same series as tenant invoices), and posted straight away.
  4. Select Invoice on any of them to print the tax invoice for the owner. Record the owner's TRN on the property (Master Data ▸ Properties, Owner TRN) if they are VAT registered, so it appears on the invoice.

The commission itself is already in revenue through revenue recognition, so the invoice only adds the VAT. It appears in the Reporting ▸ VAT Return for that month as Standard rated - management commission to landlords. Commission that was invoiced once is never invoiced again; commission recognised late (for example a month posted after its invoice run) is picked up by the next run. A company with no TRN issues the invoice without VAT.

An issued commission tax invoice cannot be reset to draft

It has a number from your tax invoice series and has been handed to the owner, so its VAT must stay in the return. Correct a mistake with an adjustment in the following month rather than by undoing the invoice.

Paying an owner

  1. Open Operations ▸ Owner Settlements. Every landlord named on a managed property is listed with the rent billed, commission, owner's share, what was paid and the balance.
  2. Select Statement & Pay for the owner. Change Statement as at if you are settling up to an earlier date, for example a month end.
  3. Check the contracts: property and unit, tenant, agreement rent, rent billed, commission % and amount, the VAT on the commission, and the owner's share. The commission tax invoices issued to the owner are listed underneath.
  4. Under Pay the Owner, the amount starts at what has been collected. Keep it to pay in full, or enter a smaller amount for a part payment.
  5. Enter the Payment date, Payment method (bank transfer, cheque or cash), the account it is Paid from, and the cheque or transfer reference.
  6. Select Save and Post Payment. The payment is posted straight away and the settlement advice opens, ready to print for the owner.

An owner can never be paid twice

The most you can pay is the balance, less any settlement still waiting to be posted. A larger amount is refused with "The most that can be paid to … is …". Settlement numbers run OS-year-0001, OS-year-0002 and so on.

Why Due to Landlords in the Trial Balance can be higher than the balances here

When an invoice is posted the whole rent goes to Due to Landlords, and our commission is taken out of it month by month by revenue recognition, with its VAT by the monthly commission invoice. The settlement statement deducts the commission and VAT on everything billed at once. So when rent is billed in advance, Due to Landlords also holds the commission and VAT for the months still to come, and is higher than the total balance payable by that amount.

Correcting a settlement

Find the settlement's journal in Operations ▸ Journal Inquiry (source OwnerSettlement) and reset it to draft. A draft settlement no longer counts as paid, but still holds back the amount until it is posted again, so nothing is paid twice by mistake.

Rent invoiced in the owner's name, and costs charged to the owner

Two lines can appear between the commission and the owner's share:

  • Add: VAT on rent invoiced in the owner's name — for a property set to invoice tenants in the owner's name, the VAT collected from tenants belongs to the owner, who declares and pays it to the FTA. It is passed on with the rent.
  • Less: maintenance and expenses charged to the owner — materials taken from the maintenance store for the owner's units (see Maintenance Jobs), and other costs booked to Due to Landlords for the owner's properties. Each is listed with its date, property and entry.

The amount that may be paid to the owner follows: it can never exceed the owner's share after these lines.
