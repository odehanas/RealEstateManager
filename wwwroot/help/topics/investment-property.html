Reporting ▸ Investment Property Note

Buildings held to earn rent are investment property. The company uses the cost model (IAS 40): investment property is carried at cost less accumulated depreciation. Land is never depreciated; buildings are depreciated straight-line over their useful lives. The fair value is not booked — it is disclosed in the note, as the standard requires.

Running the note

  1. Enter the period — normally the financial year — and select Refresh.
  2. The first table is the movement in the carrying amount: cost at the start, additions, buildings transferred from properties under construction, disposals, cost at the end; then accumulated depreciation at the start, the charge for the period and at the end; and the carrying amount at both dates.
  3. The second table shows each property's cost, accumulated depreciation, carrying amount and fair value, with the valuation date and valuer.
  4. Select Print to take it into the financial statements. A suggested wording for the accounting policy and fair value disclosure is printed underneath.

The figures are read from 15100 Investment Property and 15150 Accumulated Depreciation - Investment Property, so they agree with the balance sheet. Opening balances brought forward from a previous system, and entries posted without a property, are shown on a separate line as not allocated to a property.

Recording the fair value

Open the property in Master Data ▸ Properties ▸ Edit and fill in the Valuation section: the fair value from the latest independent valuation, its date and the valuer. Update it at each year end. If any investment property has no fair value, the note warns you.

Where the depreciation comes from

When a rental building is completed and transferred (see Development Projects), its building part is entered in the fixed asset register with the useful life you gave, and the monthly depreciation run posts the charge:

Depreciating a building brought in as an opening balance

An investment property brought in as an opening balance on 15100 is not depreciated until it is set up. At the foot of the note, under Depreciate an Existing Building (needs Manage Accounting):

  1. Choose the property and the month depreciation starts.
  2. Split its carrying amount into land (never depreciated) and building. If the building was depreciated in a previous system, enter its carrying amount — cost less the depreciation so far.
  3. Enter the building's remaining useful life in years.
  4. Leave allocate ticked to move land and building out of the unallocated opening balance and onto the property, so the note shows them against the building. Select Set Up Depreciation.

The building is entered in the fixed asset register and the monthly depreciation run charges it from then on.
