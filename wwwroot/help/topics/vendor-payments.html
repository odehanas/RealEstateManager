Operations ▸ Vendor Payments

Money paid to a supplier always settles specific bills. Each settlement gets a clearing document number, and money paid before there is a bill — an advance — is kept on its own account until it is applied to bills. The list shows every payment with its type, clearing document, the bills it settled and its status.

Paying bills

  1. Select New Vendor Payment, or Register Payment on a posted vendor bill.
  2. 1. Vendor: choose the supplier. Their posted bills with something still open are listed.
  3. 2. Payment type: leave it on Pay open bills.
  4. 3. Open items: tick the bills to pay. Pay now starts at each bill's full open amount; lower it for a part payment.
  5. 4. Payment details: the Payment date, the bank or cash account it is Paid from, the Method (bank, cheque, cash or transfer) and the cheque or transfer reference. The payment number is given automatically.
  6. Check Total to pay and select Save and Post.

The payment receives a clearing document number (CLR-year-…). A bill paid in full becomes Paid and shows the clearing document that settled it; a part-paid bill becomes PartiallyPaid and stays open for the balance. Select a payment in the list to see the bills it settled.

Only posted bills can be paid

A draft bill is not offered: it has not been added to Accounts Payable, so there is nothing to pay off. Post the bill first. A payment must settle at least one open bill — money paid before there is a bill is an advance.

Paying an advance (down payment)

  1. Select Pay an advance — or choose Advance (down payment) as the payment type.
  2. Choose the vendor and enter the Amount.
  3. Enter the payment details and select Save and Post.

The advance is an asset, not a reduction of Accounts Payable, so what you owe suppliers is never understated by advances that have not been billed yet. The list shows how much of each advance is still open.

Applying an advance to bills

When the supplier's bills arrive and are posted:

  1. Select Apply advance to bills, or Apply to bills on the advance itself.
  2. Choose the vendor and the Advance to apply, and the Clearing date.
  3. Under Open bills, enter how much of each bill to Settle from advance.
  4. Select Apply and Post.

No money moves; the application gets its own clearing document number. Pay any remainder of the bills with a normal payment. A contractor's mobilisation advance recovered over several progress bills is applied part by part, as each bill is certified.

VAT on an advance

If the supplier issues a tax invoice for the advance, record it as a vendor bill — so its input VAT is recovered — and pay that bill. Use the advance function for advances without a tax invoice.

When payment approval is on

The payment waits under Controls ▸ Payment Approvals and shows Awaiting approval / draft in the list until someone whose authority covers it releases it. Meanwhile its amounts are held against the bills, so they cannot be paid twice. See Payment Approvals & Limits.

Corrections

  • Reset the payment or the advance application to draft from Operations ▸ Journal Inquiry: the bills reopen and an advance's open amount is restored. Post again when corrected.
  • An advance that has been applied cannot be reset until its applications are reset.
  • A bill settled by a payment or an advance cannot be reset to draft until those are reset first.

Month-end checks

  • 11550 Advances to Suppliers in the Trial Balance equals the open advances on the vendor statements.
  • Accounts Payable equals the open bill balances of all vendors.
  • Review advances that have been open for long: apply them, or chase the supplier.

To see everything billed by and paid to one supplier, with their advances, use Reporting ▸ Vendor Statement.
