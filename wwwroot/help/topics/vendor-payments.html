Paying a bill

  1. Select New Payment, or Pay / Register Payment on a posted vendor bill.
  2. Choose the Vendor Bill being paid. Its outstanding balance is shown.
  3. Enter the Payment Number and the Payment Date.
  4. Enter the Amount. Part payments are allowed.
  5. Choose the Payment Method and save.
  6. Post the draft journal from Operations ▸ Journal Inquiry.

Only posted bills can be paid

The bill list shows posted bills with something left to pay. A draft bill is not offered, and if one is chosen another way the payment is refused with "Bill … has not been posted yet". A draft has not been added to Accounts Payable, so there is nothing to pay off. Post the bill first.

When the payment is posted, the bill's Amount Paid rises and its status moves to PartiallyPaid or Paid. While a payment is posted against a bill, the bill itself cannot be reset to draft — reset the payment first.
