How the system applies UAE VAT to the sale and lease of real estate, and the ten-year review of input tax on buildings. Confirm the treatment of unusual cases with your tax adviser.

Supplies: sales and leases

SupplyTreatment
Commercial property — sale or leaseStandard rated, 5%
Residential — the first supply of a new unit (its first sale or first lease), within three years of the building's completion, or before completionZero rated — the related input tax is recoverable
Residential — any later supplyExempt — no output tax, and related input tax is not recoverable

The system decides this for every new lease and sale:

  • A building is new when its property has a completion date (from the completion certificate) or a development project. Without either, a residential building is treated as existing and its supplies are exempt.
  • Each unit records its first supply — the date, whether it was a sale or a lease, and the contract — when the first contract takes effect. Every later lease or sale of the unit is exempt.
  • The three years run from the completion date. The property page shows the last day of the window.
  • A unit or property deliberately set to zero rated keeps that; a lease's treatment can still be overridden with a reason.

The Capital Assets Scheme

Operations ▸ Capital Assets Scheme (VAT)

A building costing AED 5 million or more before VAT is a capital asset. The input tax recovered on it is reviewed every year for ten years against how it was actually used. The usual case: a residential building built to rent out recovers its construction VAT because the first leases are zero rated, but later leases are exempt — so part of that VAT is paid back over the following years.

  1. Register the building. Completed rental or own-use development projects of AED 5 million or more are listed; Register takes the input VAT charged and recovered from the project's posted supplier bills. A building bought or built outside the system is registered by hand. Year 1 starts on the first-use date.
  2. Review each year once it is over. Open the asset: the next year due is offered, with a proposed taxable use worked out from the building's leases — each unit's area for the days it was let, standard or zero-rated leases counting as taxable and exempt leases as exempt. Change it if you know better.
  3. Post the adjustment. Adjustment = input VAT × (taxable use this year − taxable use at recovery) ÷ 10. It is dated after the year ends and goes in that period's VAT return.

In the VAT return

The VAT return shows unit sales by treatment (… - sale of units): payments received before handover in the period they are received, and the balance at handover in the handover period. Capital Assets Scheme adjustments appear under input tax as Capital Assets Scheme adjustment.
