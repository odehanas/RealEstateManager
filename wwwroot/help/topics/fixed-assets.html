The fixed asset register tracks long-lived items — lifts, generators, air-conditioning plant, vehicles, fit-out — from acquisition through capitalisation to monthly depreciation.

Creating an asset

  1. Select Add Fixed Asset.
  2. Enter the Asset Code, the Asset Name and the Category (furniture, equipment, vehicles …). Choose the Property it belongs to, or leave Not Property-Specific.
  3. Enter the Acquisition Date and the In Service Date.
  4. Enter the Useful Life (Months) and any Residual Value.
  5. Optionally enter the Initial Acquisition Cost with the account it was Paid / Payable From (and the Vendor, when that is Accounts Payable). Further costs are added afterwards.
  6. Check the Asset Account, the Accumulated Depreciation Account and the Depreciation Expense Account (defaults 15200, 15300 and 62100).
  7. For assets migrated from a previous system, enter the Opening Cost and Opening Accumulated Depreciation.
  8. Select Save Draft Asset.

The asset is saved as a draft. It is not depreciated until it has been capitalised.

Acquisition costs

Select Acquisition on the asset's row to accumulate the costs that make up its value — the supplier invoice, freight, installation, customs, testing. For each cost enter the Cost Date, Cost Type, Amount, whether it is Capitalizable, and the account it was Paid / Payable From, then select Add Cost. Only a draft asset accepts new costs. Choosing a vendor with an Accounts Payable offset also raises the vendor bill automatically, so the payable appears in the vendor's sub-ledger.

Capitalisation

When the asset is complete and in use, capitalise it on the same screen: enter the Capitalization Date and select Capitalize Asset. The accumulated clearing balance moves to the asset account and the depreciation schedule is generated over the useful life. Depreciation starts only after capitalisation:

Running depreciation

  1. Open Operations ▸ Fixed Assets. The Due to Post card shows how many monthly instalments are waiting.
  2. Enter the Posting Date — normally the month end being closed.
  3. Select Post Due Depreciation. Every planned instalment up to the month end of that date is posted, for all assets.

Depreciation on an asset's row shows its schedule month by month, with what has been posted and its net book value. The same run is offered as Run depreciation in the Closing Cockpit.

In this chart of accounts, 15300 is classified as an asset and carries a credit balance, acting as a contra-asset. It therefore reduces Total Assets on the Balance Sheet, which is the correct presentation. Do not "correct" its sign.
