The fixed asset register tracks long-lived items — lifts, generators, air-conditioning plant, vehicles, fit-out — from acquisition through capitalisation to monthly depreciation.

Creating an asset

  1. Select New Fixed Asset.
  2. Enter the Asset Code and Name.
  3. Select the Asset Account and the Accumulated Depreciation Account (defaults 15200 and 15300).
  4. Enter the Useful Life (months) and the Depreciation Start Date.
  5. For assets migrated from a previous system, enter the Opening Cost and Opening Accumulated Depreciation.
  6. Save.

Acquisition costs

Open an asset and select Acquisition to accumulate the costs that make up its value — the supplier invoice, freight, installation, professional fees. Each entry posts:

Choosing a vendor with an Accounts Payable offset also raises the vendor bill automatically, so the payable appears in the vendor's sub-ledger.

Capitalisation

When the asset is complete and in use, capitalise it. The accumulated clearing balance moves to the asset account and the depreciation schedule is generated over the useful life:

Running depreciation

  1. Open Depreciation from the fixed asset screen.
  2. Enter the posting date — normally the month end being closed.
  3. Select Post Depreciation. Every scheduled instalment on or before that date is posted.

Accumulated Depreciation presentation

In this chart of accounts, 15300 is classified as an asset and carries a credit balance, acting as a contra-asset. It therefore reduces Total Assets on the Balance Sheet, which is the correct presentation. Do not "correct" its sign.
