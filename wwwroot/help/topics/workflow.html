What happens at each step

StepWhereWhat it creates
1. PropertiesMaster Data ▸ PropertiesThe building record and its ownership type. No accounting.
2. UnitsMaster Data ▸ UnitsRentable units, each starting as Vacant. No accounting.
3. TenantsMaster Data ▸ TenantsThe tenant register and any opening receivable balance.
4. ContractsMaster Data ▸ LeasesThe lease, plus the complete draft invoice schedule plus the monthly revenue-recognition plan. The unit becomes Occupied.
5. PDCsOperations ▸ PDC ReceiptsA cheque record and a memorandum journal dated on the received date (PDC On Hand / PDC Contra), saved as a draft to post.
6. InvoicingOperations ▸ Customer InvoicesThe receivable, the deferred-revenue liability and any VAT payable.
7. PaymentsOperations ▸ Customer ReceiptsA draft receipt. Once posted: cash in, receivable down, invoice status advanced to Partially Paid or Paid.
The one rule that makes the flow work

You cannot skip a step, because each screen selects a record created by the previous screen. There is no unit without a property, no lease without a unit and a tenant, no invoice without a lease, and no receipt without an invoice. If a dropdown is empty, the previous step has not been completed.

Post your drafts

Steps 5 and 7 save draft journals that do not reach the reports until posted from Operations ▸ Journal Inquiry. See Draft, Post & Reset to Draft.

A typical first week

  1. Day 1 — Setup. Review the Chart of Accounts, confirm Account Mapping, enter Opening Balances and confirm they are balanced.
  2. Day 2 — Portfolio. Enter every property, then every unit under each property.
  3. Day 3 — People. Enter tenants (with opening balances if migrating) and vendors.
  4. Day 4 — Contracts. Enter each live lease. Check the generated invoice schedule after each one.
  5. Day 5 — Cheques. Log the PDCs held against each lease.
  6. Day 6 — Verify. Post every draft in Journal Inquiry, then run the Trial Balance and confirm total debit equals total credit.
