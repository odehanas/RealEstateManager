Two read-only screens for owners and managers, under the Controls menu. Business Performance answers "how is the business doing, and what is waiting for me?". The Owner Monitor answers "what happened to the money?". Neither can change anything.

Business Performance

Controls ▸ Business Performance — needs View Business Performance or Owner Oversight. All figures come from posted entries.

SectionWhat it shows
Waiting for your decisionEverything only you (or your level) can move on: contracts to approve, payments to release, period reopen requests. Review opens each one.
Revenue and net profit this yearWith expenses, against last year.
Cash and bankThe balance now, and collections this month and this year. Collections are receipts plus cleared cheques.
OccupancyUnits let and vacant, and the annual rent roll.
Owed by tenantsTotal receivable and how much is overdue, with a chart of overdue rent by age: 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days.
Cheques in handPost-dated cheques held, and those due in the next 30 days.
Owed to suppliersTotal payable and what falls due in 30 days.
Contracts ending in 90 daysEach contract, tenant, unit, end date and the annual rent to renew.
Revenue and expenses, last 12 monthsA chart month by month.

Print gives a one-page summary. The detailed reports behind each figure are under Reporting.

Owner Monitor

Controls ▸ Owner Monitor — needs Owner Oversight.

  1. Choose the From and To dates.
  2. Set Large if over to the amount above which you want a posting pointed out, and select Show.
SectionWhat it shows
Cash and bank nowThe balance, and money in and out in the period.
Payments waiting for releaseHow many and how much, with a link to the queue.
Authority delegated nowWho is acting above their level, up to how much and until when.
Needs your attentionPostings over your "large" amount, and journals left as drafts for more than a week.
Payments released and waitingEvery payment in the period: who entered it, the amount, its status and who released or decided it — or that it was released within the person's own limit.
Postings to the accountsEvery entry posted in the period, with large ones highlighted.

The Owner Monitor is about the business, not about who clicked what: the record of every user's actions is the audit log, under System Setup ▸ Audit Logs — see Subscription, Audit Log & Feedback.
