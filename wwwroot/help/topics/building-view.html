Reporting ▸ Building View, or Building view on a property's page.

Each building drawn floor by floor, every unit coloured by the health of its rent, so an owner sees at a glance where the building earns well and where it needs attention. Select a unit for its details.

All buildings

The first page shows every property as a card: how much of it is let, a bar of its units by colour, the counts of healthy, watch and at-risk units, and the rent overdue. Open building draws it.

The colours

ColourWhen
Green - healthyLet, and none of the warning signs below
Yellow - watchTenant health score below 75, or rent overdue more than 30 days, or a request to postpone a payment in the last 12 months
Red - at riskTenant health score below 55, or rent overdue more than 90 days, or a cheque on the contract bounced in the last 12 months
GreyVacant (no current contract); striped grey is under maintenance
BlueReserved for a buyer (light) or sold and handed over (darker) - see Unit Sales

The score is the tenant's score from Tenant Health & Ranking, so the drawing and the report always agree. Select a colour above the drawing to show only those units; select it again to show all.

A unit's details

Selecting a unit shows its tenant (linked to their statement) and contract, the health score and grade, the annual rent, what was billed, collected, outstanding and overdue (with the age of the oldest overdue item), bounced cheques and postponement requests in the last 12 months, the days to the contract's end (highlighted inside 90 days), a bar of billed rent collected, and Why this colour - each reason that set it. For a vacant unit it shows how long it has been empty and the rent lost.

How the building is drawn

  • Floors run from the lowest to the highest unit, and up to the property's Number of floors when recorded, so a tower with only some units entered still has its full height. Floors with no units say so.
  • Each unit is placed on its floor by the unit's Floor number; when that is empty, it is read from the Floor text (G = ground, 3 = third, B1 = first basement).
  • Along the floor, units go left to right by Position on the floor, then by unit number, and each is as wide as its share of the floor's area.

Set both on the unit in Master Data ▸ Units ▸ Edit to arrange a building exactly as it stands.
