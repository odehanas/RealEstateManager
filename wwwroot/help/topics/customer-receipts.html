Workflow step 7. A customer receipt records money collected from a tenant against a specific invoice — cash, bank transfer, or a cheque you are banking directly. Cheques handled through the PDC register clear themselves and do not need a receipt.

Recording a receipt

  1. Select New Receipt, or Receive on the invoice in Customer Invoices.
  2. Choose the Invoice being paid. Its outstanding balance is shown.
  3. Enter the Receipt Number — unique within the company.
  4. Enter the Receipt Date — the date the money actually arrived.
  5. Enter the Amount. Part payments are allowed.
  6. Choose the Payment Method and save.
Save and Post puts the receipt straight into the accounts

The invoice and the tenant's balance go down at once. If the posting is refused - for example the period is closed - the receipt is kept as a draft and a yellow message says why; post it from Operations ▸ Journal Inquiry once that is resolved. See Draft, Post & Reset to Draft.

Only posted invoices can be received against

The invoice list shows posted invoices with a balance. A draft invoice is not yet a receivable, so a receipt against one is refused with "Invoice … has not been posted yet". Post the invoice first; the Receive button on the invoice list only appears once it is posted.

Effect on the invoice

When the receipt's journal is posted, the amount is added to the invoice's Amount Paid and its status moves to PartiallyPaid or Paid. Resetting the journal to draft takes it back off again.

One receipt, one invoice

Each receipt settles a single invoice. If a tenant pays one amount covering three invoices, enter three receipts with the same date and reference, splitting the amount. This keeps the tenant statement accurate at invoice level.

If saving fails

If the posting cannot be prepared — a missing account mapping, or a date in a closed period — nothing is saved and the message tells you why. You will not be left with a half-recorded receipt.
