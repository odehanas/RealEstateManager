The tenant portal is a separate, mobile-friendly site where each tenant sees their own account and sends you requests. Tenants cannot register themselves: you issue each one a login.

Giving a tenant access

  1. Open Master Data ▸ Tenants and select Portal on the tenant's row.
  2. Under Issue Access, check the username. The tenant's email is filled in when it is on file; it must be unique across all tenants.
  3. Select Issue portal access.
  4. A green box shows the username and a temporary password. Copy both now — the password is shown only once and cannot be looked up later, by anyone.
  5. Send the tenant the address https://realestatemanager.odehanas.com/portal/login with their username and temporary password.

On first sign-in the tenant must choose their own password before going any further.

Managing an existing login

The Current Status panel shows the username, whether the account is active, whether the temporary password has been changed yet, the last sign-in, and any lock-out.

SituationWhat to do
Tenant forgot the passwordReset password issues a new temporary password and the old one stops working.
Tenant is locked outToo many wrong passwords lock the account for a while. Wait, or reset the password.
Tenancy has endedSuspend access blocks sign-in immediately. The account and its history are kept, and Re-enable access restores it.

Only users with Create & Edit Master Data can issue or change portal logins.

Tenants see posted transactions only

Invoices, receipts, cheques and deposits appear in the portal once their journal is posted, so what a tenant sees always agrees with their balance. A receipt you have saved but not yet posted does not show — post receipts and cheques the same day so tenants see their payments promptly.

Send tenants the Guide for Tenants; it is also available to them from the portal.

A tenant only ever sees their own account

Every portal screen takes the tenant from their sign-in, never from the address bar, so changing a number in the URL cannot open another tenant's data. A tenant who tries to open a staff page is sent back to their portal.

Testing it yourself

Issue a login to a test tenant and sign in as them in a private window (Ctrl + Shift + N). In the same window as your staff session, each kind of sign-in would keep sending you back to its own side.

Requests that tenants raise are handled in Tenant Requests.
