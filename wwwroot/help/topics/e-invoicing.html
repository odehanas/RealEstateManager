From 2027 every invoice and credit note a UAE business issues to another business or a government entity must be an e-invoice: a structured file in the official UAE format (PINT AE), sent through an Accredited Service Provider (ASP) that delivers it to your customer's provider and reports it to the FTA. This screen holds everything the system needs to produce those files for your company.

Who does what

This system produces each e-invoice in the official format, checks the data behind it, keeps the file and tracks what your provider said about it. Your provider validates it, delivers it and reports it to the FTA. You must appoint a provider yourself — it cannot be replaced by software.

The mandate covers every business, whether or not it is VAT registered. Invoices to individuals (B2C) are outside it for now, so a residential tenant who is a private person is not affected.

Setting up

  1. Open System Setup ▸ E-Invoicing Settings.
  2. Choose the Revenue band. The appointment deadline and start date appear underneath. Enter a start date only to join earlier, voluntarily.
  3. Complete the Company identity: registered legal name, TRN (the 10-digit TIN is taken from it), trade licence number and the authority that issued it, and the address with its emirate.
  4. Under Payment, choose how tenants pay. Bank transfer needs your IBAN, which is printed on every e-invoice.
  5. Check the Readiness check at the top. It lists anything missing from the company and from any business tenant, with a link to the record to fix.
  6. Once your provider is appointed, enter its name, tick E-invoicing is switched on and save.

Switching on re-assesses every invoice already posted: those to business and government tenants dated on or after the start date are queued in the E-Invoice Monitor.

Checked against the official rules

The files follow PINT AE Billing 1.0.4, published for the Ministry of Finance. Before release they were checked against the official validation rules and the UBL 2.1 schema — the same checks your provider runs.
